The event \(X\ge3\) happens when \(X=3\) or \(X=4\). These are different values, so the probabilities add. Both are given in the table:

\[P(X\ge3)=P(X=3)+P(X=4)=0.0756+0.0081=0.0837.\]

The unknown entries \(a\) and \(b\), and the value of \(p\), are not needed.
