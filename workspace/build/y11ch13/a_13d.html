The proportion \(\hat{p}\) is the relative frequency of the outcome 3 in the 18 rolls, that is, the number of rolls with an outcome of 3 divided by 18. So exactly 12 rolls with an outcome of 3 corresponds to \(\hat{p}=\tfrac{12}{18}=\tfrac23\), and ‘at most 12’ (12 or fewer) means \(\hat{p}\le\tfrac23\). The probability is \(P\big(\hat{p}\le\tfrac23\big)\).

Answer B. Option D is the probability of at least 12 rolls with an outcome of 3. Options A and C use \(\tfrac16\), which is the probability of a 3 on one roll, not the proportion \(\tfrac{12}{18}\).

Expected value is a measure of central tendency: it measures the centre of the data set. It may also be thought of as a weighted mean (weighted by the probabilities of the distribution). If the experiment is carried out experimentally a large number of times we would expect that the average of the outcomes would approach the expected value.
10
The standard deviation is the square root of the variance. Both measure the spread of the data, so that a distribution with a larger standard deviation is more spread out than a distribution with a smaller standard deviation. Both are zero if the distribution only takes one value, that is, if it is not spread out at all. If the distribution is stretched (multiplied) by a constant \(k\) the standard deviation also increases by a factor \(k\).
