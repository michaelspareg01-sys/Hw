1
a There are 12 court cards, so \(P(\text{court card})=\dfrac{12}{52}=\dfrac3{13}\).
b \(P(\text{not a court card})=1-\dfrac3{13}=\dfrac{10}{13}\).
c There are 13 hearts and 4 kings, and the king of hearts is counted in both. So \(P(\text{heart or king})=\dfrac{13}{52}+\dfrac4{52}-\dfrac1{52}=\dfrac{16}{52}=\dfrac4{13}\).
2
a The tosses are independent, so \(P(HHH)=\dfrac12\times\dfrac12\times\dfrac12=\dfrac18\).
b \(P(\text{at least one head})=1-P(TTT)=1-\dfrac18=\dfrac78\).
c The three outcomes with two heads are \(HHT\), \(HTH\) and \(THH\), each with probability \(\dfrac18\). So \(P(\text{exactly two heads})=\dfrac38\).
3
a The stages are independent: \(P(RR)=\dfrac35\times\dfrac35=\dfrac9{25}\).
b With no replacement, after one red is removed there are 2 red in 4: \(P(RR)=\dfrac35\times\dfrac24=\dfrac3{10}\).
4
a \(f_r=\dfrac f{50}\): \(0.12,\ 0.28,\ 0.36,\ 0.24\). Check: \(0.12+0.28+0.36+0.24=1\).
b \(\bar x=\sum x\,f_r=1(0.12)+2(0.28)+3(0.36)+4(0.24)=0.12+0.56+1.08+0.96=2.72\)
c \(\sum x^2f_r=1(0.12)+4(0.28)+9(0.36)+16(0.24)=0.12+1.12+3.24+3.84=8.32\), so \(\sigma^2=8.32-2.72^2=8.32-7.3984=0.9216\) and \(\sigma=\sqrt{0.9216}=0.96\).
