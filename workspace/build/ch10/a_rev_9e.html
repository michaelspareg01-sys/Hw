Past-Paper Homework - Worked Solutions
PP 1Caringbah High School, 2020, Higher School Certificate Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q2

\(V=\tfrac43\pi r^3\), so \(\dfrac{dV}{dr}=4\pi r^2\). Then \(\dfrac{dr}{dt}=\dfrac{dV}{dt}\div\dfrac{dV}{dr}=\dfrac{200\pi}{4\pi\times10^2}=0.5\) cm s\(^{-1}\). Answer (B).

PP 2Sydney Girls High School, 2023, Trial Higher School Certificate Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q11(d)

\(A=\pi r^2\), so \(\dfrac{dA}{dr}=2\pi r\) and

\[\dfrac{dA}{dt}=\dfrac{dA}{dr}\times\dfrac{dr}{dt}=2\pi\times8\times2=32\pi.\]

The area is increasing at \(32\pi\) cm\(^2\)/s (about 100.5 cm\(^2\)/s).

PP 3Killara High School, 2024, Year 12 Examination, Mathematics Extension 1 HSC Trial Examination, Q13(e)

Let \(x\) m be the man's distance from the closest point of the path, and \(\theta\) the angle in the diagram. Then \(\tan\theta=\dfrac{x}{6}\), and \(\dfrac{dx}{dt}=1.2\). Differentiating with respect to \(t\):

\[\sec^2\theta\,\dfrac{d\theta}{dt}=\dfrac16\,\dfrac{dx}{dt},\quad\text{so}\quad\dfrac{d\theta}{dt}=\dfrac16\cos^2\theta\,\dfrac{dx}{dt}.\]

When \(x=8\), the distance from the searchlight to the man is \(\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=10\), so \(\cos\theta=\dfrac{6}{10}=\dfrac35\). Hence

\[\dfrac{d\theta}{dt}=\dfrac16\times\dfrac{9}{25}\times1.2=\dfrac{9}{125}=0.072.\]

The searchlight is rotating at \(\dfrac{9}{125}\) radians per second.

PP 4Ascham School, 2024, Year 12 Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q14(c)

The full cone has radius 3 m and height 5 m.
By similar triangles \(\dfrac{r}{h}=\dfrac35\), so \(r=\dfrac{3h}{5}\). Then

\[V=\dfrac13\pi\left(\dfrac{3h}{5}\right)^2h=\dfrac{3\pi h^3}{25},\qquad\dfrac{dV}{dh}=\dfrac{9\pi h^2}{25}.\]

The water leaks out at 1.5 L/min, so \(\dfrac{dV}{dt}=-0.0015\) m\(^3\)/min. Then

\[\dfrac{dh}{dt}=\dfrac{dV}{dt}\div\dfrac{dV}{dh}=-0.0015\times\dfrac{25}{9\pi h^2}.\]

When \(h=1\): \(\dfrac{dh}{dt}=-\dfrac{1}{240\pi}\approx-0.00133\). The water level is falling at \(\dfrac{1}{240\pi}\approx0.00133\) m/min, that is, about 1.3 mm per minute.

PP 5North Sydney Girls High School, 2023, HSC Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q13(d)

(i) Take a vertical cross-section through \(O\) and the centre of the table. Let the shadow have radius \(\rho\) m. The lamp is \(h\) m above the table top and \(h+1\) m above the ground. The two right triangles with vertex \(O\) are similar, so

\[\dfrac{\rho}{h+1}=\dfrac{3}{h},\quad\text{so}\quad\rho=\dfrac{3(h+1)}{h}=3\left(1+\dfrac1h\right).\]

Hence \(A=\pi\rho^2=9\pi\left(1+\dfrac1h\right)^2\).

(ii) \(\dfrac{dA}{dh}=9\pi\times2\left(1+\dfrac1h\right)\times\left(-\dfrac{1}{h^2}\right)=-\dfrac{18\pi}{h^2}\left(1+\dfrac1h\right)\). When \(h=3\): \(\dfrac{dA}{dh}=-\dfrac{18\pi}{9}\times\dfrac43=-\dfrac{8\pi}{3}\).

The lamp is being lowered, so \(\dfrac{dh}{dt}=-\dfrac{3}{100}\). Then

\[\dfrac{dA}{dt}=\dfrac{dA}{dh}\times\dfrac{dh}{dt}=-\dfrac{8\pi}{3}\times\left(-\dfrac{3}{100}\right)=\dfrac{2\pi}{25}.\]

The area of the shadow is increasing at \(\dfrac{2\pi}{25}\) m\(^2\)/s.

PP 6North Sydney Boys High School, 2022, HSC Course Assessment Task 3-Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q12(b)

(i) The centre of the sphere lies on the axis of the cone, \(R\) below the vertex, so it is \(|2-R|\) from the plane of the base. A point on the rim of the base is on the sphere, so by Pythagoras

\[r^2+(2-R)^2=R^2,\quad\text{so}\quad r^2=4R-4.\]

For the slant height, \(l^2=r^2+2^2=4R\). Hence

\[A=\pi rl=\pi\sqrt{4R-4}\,\sqrt{4R}=4\pi\sqrt{(R-1)R}=4\pi\sqrt{R^2-R}.\]

(ii) The volume of the sphere is \(V=\tfrac43\pi R^3\), so \(\dfrac{dV}{dR}=4\pi R^2\).

Then \(\dfrac{dR}{dt}=\dfrac{8}{4\pi R^2}=\dfrac{2}{\pi R^2}\). When \(R=2\), \(\dfrac{dR}{dt}=\dfrac{1}{2\pi}\).

Also \(A=4\pi\left(R^2-R\right)^{\frac12}\), so \(\dfrac{dA}{dR}=4\pi\times\dfrac12\left(R^2-R\right)^{-\frac12}(2R-1)=\dfrac{2\pi(2R-1)}{\sqrt{R^2-R}}\). When \(R=2\), \(\dfrac{dA}{dR}=\dfrac{6\pi}{\sqrt2}=3\sqrt2\,\pi\). Hence

\[\dfrac{dA}{dt}=\dfrac{dA}{dR}\times\dfrac{dR}{dt}=3\sqrt2\,\pi\times\dfrac{1}{2\pi}=\dfrac{3\sqrt2}{2}.\]

The curved surface area is increasing at \(\dfrac{3\sqrt2}{2}\) units\(^2\)/sec.
