C3
Tutor demo. a \(x=2\cot\theta\), so \(\dfrac{dx}{d\theta}=-2\csc^2\theta=-\dfrac{2}{\sin^2\theta}\).   b \(\dfrac{dx}{dt}=-600\), so \(\dfrac{d\theta}{dt}=-\dfrac{\sin^2\theta}{2}\times(-600)=300\sin^2\theta=75\) radians per hour, which is \(75\times\dfrac{180}{\pi}\div3600\doteq1.19^\circ\) per second.
Your turn. \(x=\dfrac{80}{\tan\theta}\), \(\dfrac{dx}{d\theta}=-\dfrac{80}{\sin^2\theta}\), \(\dfrac{dx}{dt}=-4\), so \(\dfrac{d\theta}{dt}=\dfrac{\sin^2\theta}{20}=0.0125\) rad/s \(\doteq0.72^\circ\) per second (increasing).
