Past-Paper Homework - Worked Solutions
PP 1Baulkham Hills High School, 2023, Higher School Certificate Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q7

Take the origin at \(O\), the foot of the tower. Then \(\ddot y=-g\). The ball is thrown horizontally, so \(\dot y=0\) and \(y=90\) when \(t=0\). Integrating twice, \(\dot y=-gt\) and \(y=90-\tfrac12gt^2\).

It strikes the ground when \(y=0\), so \(t^2=\dfrac{180}{g}\) and \(t=\sqrt{\dfrac{180}{g}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{36\times5}{g}}=6\sqrt{\dfrac{5}{g}}\) seconds. The horizontal speed of 50 ms\(^{-1}\) does not affect the time. Answer (C).

PP 2The King's School, 2021, Year 12 Alternative Higher School Certificate Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q11(e)

(i) Differentiating, \(\dot x=30\sqrt3\) and \(\dot y=30-10t\). When \(t=0\), \(\dot x=30\sqrt3\) and \(\dot y=30\). If \(\theta\) is the angle of projection, then

\[\tan\theta=\dfrac{\dot y}{\dot x}=\dfrac{30}{30\sqrt3}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3},\quad\text{so}\quad\theta=30^\circ.\]

(ii) The initial speed \(V\) satisfies \(V^2=\dot x^2+\dot y^2=\left(30\sqrt3\right)^2+30^2=3600\), so \(V=60\) m/s.

PP 3North Sydney Girls High School, 2020, HSC Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q13(d)(i)(ii)

(i) Resolving the initial velocity, \(\tv{u}=30\cos\alpha^\circ\,\tv{i}+30\sin\alpha^\circ\,\tv{j}\).

(ii) In components, \(\ddot x=0\) and \(\ddot y=-10\). Integrating, \(\tv{v}=\tv{c}-10t\,\tv{j}\) for some constant vector \(\tv{c}\). When \(t=0\), \(\tv{v}=\tv{u}\), so \(\tv{c}=\tv{u}\) and

\[\tv{v}=30\cos\alpha^\circ\,\tv{i}+\left(30\sin\alpha^\circ-10t\right)\tv{j}.\]

Integrating again, \(\tv{r}=30t\cos\alpha^\circ\,\tv{i}+\left(30t\sin\alpha^\circ-5t^2\right)\tv{j}+\tv{d}\) for some constant vector \(\tv{d}\). The origin is on the ground below the point of projection, so when \(t=0\), \(\tv{r}=20\tv{j}\). Hence \(\tv{d}=20\tv{j}\) and

\[\tv{r}=30t\cos\alpha^\circ\,\tv{i}+\left(30t\sin\alpha^\circ-5t^2+20\right)\tv{j}.\]
PP 4Ascham School, 2024, Year 12 Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q12(d)

(i) He lands on the top of the pad, where \(y=1\). So \(5\left(1+t-t^2\right)=1\), that is, \(5t^2-5t-4=0\). Taking the positive root,

\[t=\dfrac{5+\sqrt{105}}{10}\approx1.5247,\]

so the time of flight is approximately 1.52 seconds.

(ii) At this time, \(x=5\sqrt3\times\dfrac{5+\sqrt{105}}{10}\approx13.2042\). The pad should be centred 13.20 m from \(O\).

(iii) Differentiating, \(\dot x=5\sqrt3\) and \(\dot y=5(1-2t)\). At impact, \(1-2t=-\dfrac{\sqrt{105}}{5}\), so \(\dot y=-\sqrt{105}\). Then

\[v^2=\dot x^2+\dot y^2=75+105=180,\quad\text{so}\quad v=6\sqrt5\approx13.42.\]

His impact speed is about 13 m/s.

(iv) At the highest point \(\dot y=0\), so \(t=\dfrac12\). Then \(y=5\left(1+\dfrac12-\dfrac14\right)=\dfrac{25}{4}=6.25\) m. He misses the ceiling by \(8-6.25=1.75\) m.

PP 5Knox Grammar School, 2023, Higher School Certificate Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q12(b)

(i) Since \(\tan\alpha=\dfrac{8}{15}\), the velocity triangle at \(B\) has sides 8, 15 and \(\sqrt{8^2+15^2}=17\). So \(\sin\alpha=\dfrac{8}{17}\) and \(\cos\alpha=\dfrac{15}{17}\). The firework has not reached its maximum height, so it is still rising, and at \(B\)

\[\dot x=34\times\dfrac{15}{17}=30,\qquad\dot y=34\times\dfrac{8}{17}=16.\]

Differentiating \(\tv{r}\), \(\dot x=V\cos\theta\) and \(\dot y=V\sin\theta-10t\). At \(t=2\), \(V\sin\theta-20=16\), so \(V\sin\theta=36\).

(ii) \(\dot x\) is constant, so \(V\cos\theta=30\). Dividing, \(\tan\theta=\dfrac{36}{30}=\dfrac65\), so \(\theta\approx50^\circ12'\). Squaring and adding,

\[V^2=36^2+30^2=2196,\quad\text{so}\quad V=\sqrt{2196}=6\sqrt{61}\approx46.86.\]

The initial speed is \(6\sqrt{61}\approx46.86\) m s\(^{-1}\) and the angle of projection is about \(50^\circ12'\).
