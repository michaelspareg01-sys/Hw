C3
Tutor demo.
a The primitive of \(12t^3\) is \(\dfrac{12t^4}{4}=3t^4\). \[\int_2^x12t^3\,dt=\Big[3t^4\Big]_2^x=3x^4-3\times2^4=3x^4-48,\] \[\begin{aligned}\text{so}\quad\frac{d}{dx}\int_2^x12t^3\,dt&=\frac{d}{dx}\big(3x^4-48\big)\\&=12x^3,\quad\text{consistent with the differential form.}\end{aligned}\]
b The primitive of \(t^3-6t^2+4\) is \(\dfrac{t^4}{4}-\dfrac{6t^3}{3}+4t=\tfrac14t^4-2t^3+4t\). \[\begin{aligned}\int_{-1}^x(t^3-6t^2+4)\,dt&=\Big[\tfrac14t^4-2t^3+4t\Big]_{-1}^x\\&=\big(\tfrac14x^4-2x^3+4x\big)-\big(\tfrac14(-1)^4-2(-1)^3+4(-1)\big)\\&=\big(\tfrac14x^4-2x^3+4x\big)-\big(\tfrac14+2-4\big)\\&=\tfrac14x^4-2x^3+4x+\tfrac74,\end{aligned}\] \[\begin{aligned}\text{so}\quad\frac{d}{dx}\int_{-1}^x(t^3-6t^2+4)\,dt&=\frac{d}{dx}\big(\tfrac14x^4-2x^3+4x+\tfrac74\big)\\&=x^3-6x^2+4,\quad\text{consistent.}\end{aligned}\]
c Write \(\dfrac{1}{t^3}=t^{-3}\). Its primitive is \(\dfrac{t^{-2}}{-2}=-\tfrac12t^{-2}\). \[\int_2^x\frac{1}{t^3}\,dt=\Big[-\tfrac12t^{-2}\Big]_2^x=-\tfrac12x^{-2}-\big(-\tfrac12\times2^{-2}\big)=-\tfrac12x^{-2}+\tfrac18,\] \[\begin{aligned}\text{so}\quad\frac{d}{dx}\int_2^x\frac{1}{t^3}\,dt&=\frac{d}{dx}\big(-\tfrac12x^{-2}+\tfrac18\big)\\&=-\tfrac12\times(-2)x^{-3}\\&=x^{-3},\quad\text{consistent.}\end{aligned}\]
Your turn.
a The primitive of \(3t^2\) is \(\dfrac{3t^3}{3}=t^3\). \[\int_1^x3t^2\,dt=\Big[t^3\Big]_1^x=x^3-1^3=x^3-1,\] \[\begin{aligned}\text{so}\quad\frac{d}{dx}\int_1^x3t^2\,dt&=\frac{d}{dx}\big(x^3-1\big)\\&=3x^2,\quad\text{consistent with the differential form.}\end{aligned}\]
b The primitive of \(t^3+3t^2-2\) is \(\dfrac{t^4}{4}+\dfrac{3t^3}{3}-2t=\tfrac14t^4+t^3-2t\). \[\begin{aligned}\int_{-1}^x(t^3+3t^2-2)\,dt&=\Big[\tfrac14t^4+t^3-2t\Big]_{-1}^x\\&=\big(\tfrac14x^4+x^3-2x\big)-\big(\tfrac14(-1)^4+(-1)^3-2(-1)\big)\\&=\big(\tfrac14x^4+x^3-2x\big)-\big(\tfrac14-1+2\big)\\&=\tfrac14x^4+x^3-2x-\tfrac54,\end{aligned}\] \[\begin{aligned}\text{so}\quad\frac{d}{dx}\int_{-1}^x(t^3+3t^2-2)\,dt&=\frac{d}{dx}\big(\tfrac14x^4+x^3-2x-\tfrac54\big)\\&=x^3+3x^2-2,\quad\text{consistent.}\end{aligned}\]
c Write \(\dfrac{2}{t^2}=2t^{-2}\). Its primitive is \(\dfrac{2t^{-1}}{-1}=-2t^{-1}\). \[\int_1^x\frac{2}{t^2}\,dt=\Big[-2t^{-1}\Big]_1^x=-2x^{-1}-\big(-2\times1^{-1}\big)=-2x^{-1}+2,\] \[\begin{aligned}\text{so}\quad\frac{d}{dx}\int_1^x\frac{2}{t^2}\,dt&=\frac{d}{dx}\big(-2x^{-1}+2\big)\\&=-2\times(-1)x^{-2}\\&=2x^{-2},\quad\text{consistent.}\end{aligned}\]
