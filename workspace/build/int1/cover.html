This is the first of three integration packs. Part 2 covers exponential, logarithmic and trigonometric integrals, and Part 3 covers the Extension 1 methods and volumes of rotation. Each lesson runs in the same order: concept → tutor demo → your attempt → worked example to keep for homework. Then do the textbook homework and finish with the past-paper set under exam conditions. Answers and full past-paper solutions are in the separate Homework Answers booklet.
