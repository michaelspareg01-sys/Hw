f \(x\sqrt x=x^1\times x^{\frac12}=x^{\frac32}\)

(also written \(5x^{-1\frac12}\))

(also written \(8x^{2\frac12}\))

Year 11 Exercise 8E Q5
a \(y=\dfrac{x^3}{x}+\dfrac{x^2}{x}=x^2+x\), so \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=2x+1\).
b \(y=\dfrac{x^3}{x^5}+\dfrac{x^2}{x^5}=x^{-2}+x^{-3}\), so \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=-2x^{-3}-3x^{-4}\).
c \(y=\dfrac{4x}{x^2}-\dfrac{5x^4}{x^2}=4x^{-1}-5x^2\), so \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=-4x^{-2}-10x\).
d \(y=\dfrac{6x^2}{2x^6}+\dfrac{6x^6}{2x^6}=3x^{-4}+3\), so \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=-12x^{-5}\).

Year 11 Exercise 8G Q8
a Let \(u=5-x^2\). Then \(y=u^3\), \(\dfrac{du}{dx}=-2x\) and \(\dfrac{dy}{du}=3u^2\).
\(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{dy}{du}\times\dfrac{du}{dx}=3(5-x^2)^2\times(-2x)=-6x(5-x^2)^2\)
b Let \(u=3x^2+7\). Then \(y=u^7\), \(\dfrac{du}{dx}=6x\) and \(\dfrac{dy}{du}=7u^6\).
\(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{dy}{du}\times\dfrac{du}{dx}=7(3x^2+7)^6\times6x=42x(3x^2+7)^6\)
c Let \(u=3x^3-7\). Then \(y=u^5\), \(\dfrac{du}{dx}=9x^2\) and \(\dfrac{dy}{du}=5u^4\).
\(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{dy}{du}\times\dfrac{du}{dx}=5(3x^3-7)^4\times9x^2=45x^2(3x^3-7)^4\)
d Let \(u=x^3-x^2\). Then \(y=u^{-5}\), \(\dfrac{du}{dx}=3x^2-2x\) and \(\dfrac{dy}{du}=-5u^{-6}\).
\(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{dy}{du}\times\dfrac{du}{dx}=-5(x^3-x^2)^{-6}\times(3x^2-2x)=-5(3x^2-2x)(x^3-x^2)^{-6}\)
