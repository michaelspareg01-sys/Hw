The shaded region runs from \(x=2\) to \(x=4\), so one application of the trapezoidal rule uses the two heights \(\log_a2\) and \(\log_a4\), with width \(4-2=2\).

\[\begin{aligned}\text{Area}&\doteqdot\frac{4-2}{2}\big(\log_a2+\log_a4\big)\\&=\log_a2+\log_a4\\&=\log_a2+\log_a2^2\\&=\log_a2+2\log_a2\\&=3\log_a2.\end{aligned}\]

Answer (A). Option (C) is the average of the two heights, without multiplying by the width 2. Option (B) is wrong because \(\log_a2+\log_a4=\log_a(2\times4)=\log_a8\), not \(\log_a6\). Option (D) does not come from the two heights.
