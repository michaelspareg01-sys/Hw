This pack follows on from the angle work of Year 8 chapter 6. It covers congruent figures, the four tests for congruent triangles, and proofs that use congruent triangles to establish the properties of the special quadrilaterals. Each lesson runs in the same order: concept → tutor demo → your attempt → worked example to keep for homework. The homework comes in three bands, Easy, Medium and Hard, and each lesson ends with a past-paper set to do under exam conditions. Answers and full past-paper solutions are in the separate Homework Answers booklet.

The lessons follow Cambridge Maths NSW Stage 4 Year 8 (second edition), chapter 8 Transformations and congruence. Sections 8A–8C (reflection, translation and rotation) are revised on the next pages. Sections 8F and 8G (similar figures and similar triangles) are extension sections of the chapter and are not taught in this pack. The past-paper sets use Year 9 and Year 10 yearly examinations from NSW schools.
