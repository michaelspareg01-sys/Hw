Tutor demo.
a In \(\triangle PSM\) and \(\triangle RQM\):
\(\angle SPM=\angle QRM\) (alternate angles in parallel lines, \(PS\parallel QR\))
\(\angle PSM=\angle RQM\) (alternate angles in parallel lines, \(PS\parallel QR\))
\(PS=RQ\) (given: opposite sides of a parallelogram)
\(\therefore\triangle PSM\equiv\triangle RQM\) (AAS)
b \(\therefore PM=RM\) and \(SM=QM\) (matching sides of congruent triangles)
\(\therefore\) the diagonals \(PR\) and \(QS\) bisect each other.
Your turn.
a In \(\triangle AXE\) and \(\triangle CYE\):
\(AX=CY\) (given)
\(\angle XAE=\angle YCE\) (alternate angles in parallel lines, \(AB\parallel DC\))
\(AE=CE\) (the diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other)
\(\therefore\triangle AXE\equiv\triangle CYE\) (SAS)
The angle at \(A\) is between \(AX\) and \(AE\), and the angle at \(C\) is between \(CY\) and \(CE\): the included angles.
b \(\therefore XE=YE\) (matching sides of congruent triangles)

Year 8 Exercise 8H Q17
a In \(\triangle ABD\) and \(\triangle CDB\):
\(AB=CD\) (given)
\(AD=CB\) (given)
\(BD\) is a common side
\(\therefore\triangle ABD\equiv\triangle CDB\) (SSS)
\(\therefore\angle ABD=\angle CDB\) and \(\angle ADB=\angle CBD\) (matching angles of congruent triangles)
These are pairs of alternate angles, so \(AB\parallel DC\) and \(AD\parallel BC\). The quadrilateral has two pairs of parallel sides.
b In \(\triangle ABE\) and \(\triangle CDE\):
\(AE=CE\) (given)
\(BE=DE\) (given)
\(\angle AEB=\angle CED\) (vertically opposite angles)
\(\therefore\triangle ABE\equiv\triangle CDE\) (SAS)
\(\therefore\angle ABE=\angle CDE\) (matching angles of congruent triangles). These are alternate angles, so \(AB\parallel DC\).
In the same way, \(\triangle ADE\equiv\triangle CBE\) (SAS: \(AE=CE\), \(DE=BE\) and the vertically opposite angles \(\angle AED=\angle CEB\)), so \(\angle DAE=\angle BCE\), and these alternate angles give \(AD\parallel BC\). So \(ABCD\) is a parallelogram.
c In \(\triangle ABD\) and \(\triangle BAC\):
\(AB\) is a common side
\(AD=BC\) (opposite sides of a parallelogram)
\(BD=AC\) (given)
\(\therefore\triangle ABD\equiv\triangle BAC\) (SSS)
\(\therefore\angle DAB=\angle CBA\) (matching angles of congruent triangles)
Since \(AD\parallel BC\), \(\angle DAB+\angle CBA=180^\circ\) (cointerior angles), so both angles are \(90^\circ\). A parallelogram with a right angle is a rectangle.

PP 1Normanhurst Boys High School, 2025, Year 9 Assessment Task 4 (Yearly Examination), Mathematics, Q8(a)

i. \(\angle YWZ=90^\circ\) (given), so \(\angle XWY=180^\circ-90^\circ=90^\circ\) (angles on a straight line).
In \(\triangle XYW\) and \(\triangle ZYW\):
\(\angle XWY=\angle ZWY=90^\circ\) (shown above)
\(XY=ZY\) (given)
\(YW\) is a common side
\(\therefore\triangle XYW\equiv\triangle ZYW\) (RHS)

ii. \(XW=ZW\) (matching sides of congruent triangles \(XYW\) and \(ZYW\)), so \(W\) is the midpoint of \(XZ\): \(YW\) bisects \(XZ\).

PP 2North Sydney Boys High School, 2020, Year 9 Yearly Examination, Mathematics, Q3(b)

(i) In \(\Delta ADF\) and \(\Delta ABE\):
\(\angle ADF=\angle ABE=90^\circ\) (angles of a square)
\(AD=AB\) (sides of a square are equal)
\(DF=BE\) (\(DF=DC-FC=14-4=10\) cm and \(BE=BC-EC=14-4=10\) cm)
\(\therefore\Delta ADF\equiv\Delta ABE\) (SAS)
The right angle at \(D\) is between \(AD\) and \(DF\), and the right angle at \(B\) is between \(AB\) and \(BE\): the included angles.

(ii) Area of \(AECF\) \(=\) area of \(ABCD\) \(-\) area of \(\Delta ADF\) \(-\) area of \(\Delta ABE\)
\(=14\times14-\tfrac12\times14\times10-\tfrac12\times14\times10\)
\(=196-70-70\)
\(=56\) cm².

PP 3Sydney Technical High School, 2024, Year 9 Yearly Examination, Mathematics, Q16(c)

i. In \(\triangle POQ\) and \(\triangle ROQ\):
\(PO=RO\) (radii)
\(OQ\) is a common side
\(PQ=RQ\) (given)
\(\therefore\triangle POQ\equiv\triangle ROQ\) (SSS)

ii. Let \(QO\) extended meet the circle at \(S\), and let \(\angle OPQ=\alpha\).
\(\angle OQP=\angle OPQ=\alpha\) (equal angles opposite equal sides, \(OQ=OP\), radii)
\(\angle OQR=\angle OQP=\alpha\) (matching angles of congruent triangles), so \(\angle PQR=2\alpha\).
\(\angle ORQ=\angle OPQ=\alpha\) (matching angles of congruent triangles)
\(\angle POS=\angle OPQ+\angle OQP=2\alpha\) (exterior angle of \(\triangle POQ\))
\(\angle SOR=\angle ORQ+\angle OQR=2\alpha\) (exterior angle of \(\triangle ROQ\))
\(\therefore\angle POR=\angle POS+\angle SOR=4\alpha=2\angle PQR\)

PP 4Merewether High School, 2023, Year 10 Assessment Task 4, Mathematics, Section III Part B Q4

(i) In \(\triangle ABC\) and \(\triangle BAD\):
\(AB\) is a common side
\(BC=AD\) (given)
\(AC=BD\) (given)
\(\therefore\triangle ABC\equiv\triangle BAD\) (SSS)

(ii) \(\angle CAB=\angle DBA\) (matching angles of congruent triangles), that is, \(\angle PAB=\angle PBA\). So \(\triangle ABP\) has two equal angles: it is isosceles, with \(AP=BP\).

(iii) \(AC=BD\) (given) and \(AP=BP\) (part (ii)), so \(CP=AC-AP=BD-BP=DP\). So \(\triangle CDP\) has two equal sides: it is isosceles.
