PP 10Moriah College, 2023, Trial Higher School Certificate Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q12(a)

(i) There are \(8+k\) marbles, and 3 of them are blue, so \(P(\text{blue})=\dfrac{3}{8+k}\).

(ii) The marble is replaced each time, so \(X\sim B\big(4,\tfrac{3}{8+k}\big)\), with \(q=1-\dfrac{3}{8+k}=\dfrac{5+k}{8+k}\). Then

\[\text{Var}(X)=npq=4\times\frac{3}{8+k}\times\frac{5+k}{8+k}=\frac{12(5+k)}{(8+k)^2}.\]

Multiplying the inequality by \((8+k)^2>0\),

\[\begin{aligned}\frac{12(5+k)}{(8+k)^2}&<0.8\\60+12k&<0.8(64+16k+k^2)\\0&<0.8k^2+0.8k-8.8\\k^2+k-11&>0.\end{aligned}\]

The roots of \(k^2+k-11=0\) are \(k=\dfrac{-1\pm\sqrt{45}}{2}\), that is, \(k\doteq-3.85\) and \(k\doteq2.85\). Since \(k\ge0\), we need \(k>2.85\), and \(k\) is a whole number, so the smallest value is \(k=3\).

(Check: \(k=2\) gives \(\text{Var}(X)=\tfrac{12\times7}{100}=0.84\), and \(k=3\) gives \(\text{Var}(X)=\tfrac{12\times8}{121}\doteq0.793\).)
