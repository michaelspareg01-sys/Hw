\(P(3\le X\le7)=P(-2.5\le Z\le2.5)=2\Phi(2.5)-1\doteq2\times0.9938-1=0.9876\).

\(P(X\ge-39)=P(Z\ge-1.3)=\Phi(1.3)\doteq0.9032\).
