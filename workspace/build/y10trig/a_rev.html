Angles are rounded only at the last step. The sign \(\doteqdot\) means "approximately equal to".

1
Year 11 Advanced Exercise 5B Q4
In \(\Delta XYZ\) the right angle is at \(Y\). From \(\theta\), the side \(YZ=61\) is opposite and \(XY=78\) is adjacent.
\(\begin{aligned}[t]\tan\theta&=\frac{61}{78}\\ \theta&\doteqdot38.03^\circ\end{aligned}\)
The bearing is measured clockwise from north, and \(XY\) points north, so the bearing of \(Z\) from \(X\) is 038°T.

Year 11 Advanced Exercise 5B Q7
Let \(S\) be the start, \(E\) the end of the 120 km leg, and \(F\) the point due south of \(S\). The angle between north and \(SE\) is 130°, so the angle between south and \(SE\) is \(\angle FSE=180^\circ-130^\circ=50^\circ\). In the right-angled \(\Delta SFE\), \(SF\) is adjacent to the 50° angle and \(SE=120\) km is the hypotenuse.
\(\begin{aligned}[t]\frac{SF}{120}&=\cos50^\circ\\ SF&=120\cos50^\circ\\ &\doteqdot77.13\end{aligned}\)
She is about 77 km from her starting point.

Year 11 Advanced Exercise 5B Q8
The horizontal at \(W\) is parallel to the street, so \(\angle WCB=73^\circ\) (alternate angles). In \(\Delta WBC\), \(WB\) is opposite this angle and \(BC=7\) m is adjacent.
\(\begin{aligned}[t]\frac{WB}{7}&=\tan73^\circ\\ WB&=7\tan73^\circ\\ &\doteqdot22.90\end{aligned}\)
The window is about 23 m above the street.

a AAS: sine rule. The side \(x\) is opposite 57° and the side 23 is opposite 43°.
\(\dfrac{x}{\sin57^\circ}=\dfrac{23}{\sin43^\circ}\), so \(x=\dfrac{23\sin57^\circ}{\sin43^\circ}\doteqdot\) 28.3
b Right-angled: \(x\) is opposite 52° and 13.5 is adjacent.
\(\dfrac{x}{13.5}=\tan52^\circ\), so \(x=13.5\tan52^\circ\doteqdot\) 17.3
c SAS: cosine rule, with the included angle 62°.
\(x^2=11^2+13^2-2\times11\times13\times\cos62^\circ\)\({}=290-286\cos62^\circ\doteqdot155.73\), so \(x\doteqdot\) 12.5

\(\dfrac{x}{24}=\dfrac{1}{\sin41^\circ30'}\), so \(x=\dfrac{24}{\sin41^\circ30'}\doteqdot\) 36.2

\(x^2=7.4^2+9.2^2-2\times7.4\times9.2\times\cos98^\circ\)\({}=139.4-136.16\cos98^\circ\doteqdot158.35\), so \(x\doteqdot\) 12.6

Year 11 Advanced Exercise 5K Q2
a SSS: cosine rule. The angle \(\theta\) is between the sides 10 and 12, and opposite 11.
\(\cos\theta=\dfrac{10^2+12^2-11^2}{2\times10\times12}=\dfrac{123}{240}\), so \(\theta\doteqdot59.17^\circ\doteqdot\) 59°
b Sine rule: \(\theta\) is opposite 8, and 67° is opposite 9.
\(\dfrac{\sin\theta}{8}=\dfrac{\sin67^\circ}{9}\), so \(\sin\theta=\dfrac{8\sin67^\circ}{9}\doteqdot0.8182\) and \(\theta\doteqdot54.91^\circ\doteqdot\) 55°.
\(\theta\) is opposite the side 8, which is shorter than the side 9 opposite 67°, so \(\theta\lt67^\circ\) and \(\theta\) cannot be obtuse.
c Right-angled: 13 is adjacent to \(\theta\) and 17 is the hypotenuse.
\(\cos\theta=\dfrac{13}{17}\), so \(\theta\doteqdot40.12^\circ\doteqdot\) 40°

a \(\angle ABC\) is between \(AB=4\) and \(BC=7\), and opposite \(CA=5\).
\(\begin{aligned}[t]5^2&=4^2+7^2-2\times4\times7\times\cos B\\ 56\cos B&=16+49-25=40\\ \cos B&=\frac{40}{56}=\frac57\\ B&\doteqdot44.415^\circ\end{aligned}\)
\(0.415^\circ\times60\doteqdot25'\), so \(\angle ABC\doteqdot\) 44°25′.
b \(\text{area}=\tfrac12\times AB\times BC\times\sin B\)\({}=\tfrac12\times4\times7\times\sin44^\circ25'\)\({}=14\sin44^\circ25'\doteqdot9.80\), so the area is about 10 cm².
