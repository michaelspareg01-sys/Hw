This pack follows Topic 11 (Trigonometry C) and covers Trigonometry D (MA5-TRG-P-02). All angles are in degrees. Each lesson runs in the same order: concept → tutor demo → your attempt → worked example to keep for homework. The homework comes in four bands: Easy, Medium and Hard questions, then a past-paper mini exam to do under exam conditions. Answers and full past-paper solutions are in the separate Homework Answers booklet.
