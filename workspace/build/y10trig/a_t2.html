For \(0^\circ\lt x\lt90^\circ\), \(\sin x\gt0\) and \(\cos x\gt0\), so both logarithms exist. By the log law \(\log_a m-\log_a n=\log_a\dfrac mn\), the equation is \(\log_{\sqrt3}\dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}=1\), that is, \(\log_{\sqrt3}(\tan x)=1\). So \(\tan x=\big(\sqrt3\big)^1=\sqrt3\). In the first quadrant the related angle is the angle itself, so \(x=60^\circ\) (the school’s answer).
