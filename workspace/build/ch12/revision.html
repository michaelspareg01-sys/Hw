Trigonometric calculus
\[\dfrac{d}{dx}\sin x=\cos x\qquad \dfrac{d}{dx}\cos x=-\sin x\qquad \dfrac{d}{dx}\tan x=\sec^2x\] \[\int\cos(ax+b)\,dx=\tfrac1a\sin(ax+b)+C\qquad \int\sin(ax+b)\,dx=-\tfrac1a\cos(ax+b)+C\] \[\int\sec^2(ax+b)\,dx=\tfrac1a\tan(ax+b)+C\]
The inverse trigonometric functions
FunctionDomainRangeSymmetry
\(y=\sin^{-1}x\)\(-1\le x\le 1\)\(-\tfrac{\pi}{2}\le y\le\tfrac{\pi}{2}\)odd
\(y=\cos^{-1}x\)\(-1\le x\le 1\)\(0\le y\le\pi\)point symmetry in \((0,\tfrac{\pi}{2})\)
\(y=\tan^{-1}x\)all real \(x\)\(-\tfrac{\pi}{2}< y<\tfrac{\pi}{2}\)odd

\(\sin^{-1}(-x)=-\sin^{-1}x\), \(\cos^{-1}(-x)=\pi-\cos^{-1}x\), \(\tan^{-1}(-x)=-\tan^{-1}x\).

Areas and the reverse chain rule

Area between a curve and the \(x\)-axis: \(\displaystyle A=\int_a^b y\,dx\) (take absolute values of parts below the axis).

\[\int f'(x)\,\big(f(x)\big)^n\,dx=\dfrac{\big(f(x)\big)^{n+1}}{n+1}+C\qquad \int\dfrac{f'(x)}{f(x)}\,dx=\ln|f(x)|+C\qquad \int f'(x)e^{f(x)}\,dx=e^{f(x)}+C\]
