PP 1North Sydney Girls High School, 2023, HSC Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q1

\(\dfrac{d}{dx}\tan^{-1}(2x)=\dfrac{2}{1+(2x)^2}=\dfrac{2}{1+4x^2}\). Answer D. C forgets the factor 2 from the chain rule; A and B confuse \(\tan^{-1}\) with a power or with \(\tan\).

PP 2The King's School, 2021, Year 12 Alternative Higher School Certificate Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q4
\[y'=2x\sin^{-1}(2x)+x^2\times\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{1-4x^2}}=2x\sin^{-1}(2x)+\dfrac{2x^2}{\sqrt{1-4x^2}}\]

Answer (A). (B) drops the factor 2; (C) and (D) square \(2x\) incorrectly as \(2x^2\).

PP 3Normanhurst Boys' High School, 2021, Mathematics Extension 1 (Year 12 Course) Assessment Task 4 (Trial Examination), Q5
\[y'=-\left(-\dfrac{\frac1x}{\sqrt{1-(\ln x)^2}}\right)=\dfrac{1}{x\sqrt{(1+\ln x)(1-\ln x)}}\]

Answer (D). The minus signs cancel, and \(1-(\ln x)^2\) factorises as a difference of squares (it is not \(1-2\ln x\)).
