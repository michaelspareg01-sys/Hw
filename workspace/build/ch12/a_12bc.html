\[\int_0^{\frac13}\dfrac{-1}{\sqrt{\frac19-x^2}}\,dx=\Big[\cos^{-1}3x\Big]_0^{\frac13}=\cos^{-1}1-\cos^{-1}0=0-\tfrac{\pi}{2}=-\tfrac{\pi}{2}\]

PP 5Gosford High School, 2022, Trial Higher School Certificate Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q11(f)
\[f(x)=\int\dfrac{-3}{\sqrt{16-9x^2}}\,dx=3\times\tfrac13\cos^{-1}\dfrac{3x}{4}+C=\cos^{-1}\dfrac{3x}{4}+C\]

At \((0,-\tfrac{\pi}{2})\): \(\tfrac{\pi}{2}+C=-\tfrac{\pi}{2}\), so \(C=-\pi\) and \(f(x)=\cos^{-1}\dfrac{3x}{4}-\pi\). (Equivalently \(f(x)=-\sin^{-1}\dfrac{3x}{4}-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\).)

PP 6Fort Street High School, 2020, Trial Higher School Certificate Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q12(d)

(i)

\[\dfrac{d}{dx}\left(x\sin^{-1}x+\sqrt{1-x^2}\right)=\sin^{-1}x+\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}+\dfrac{-2x}{2\sqrt{1-x^2}}=\sin^{-1}x\]

(ii)

\[\int_0^1 2\sin^{-1}x\,dx=2\Big[x\sin^{-1}x+\sqrt{1-x^2}\Big]_0^1=2\left(\tfrac{\pi}{2}+0-0-1\right)=\pi-2\]
PP 7Hornsby Girls High School, 2022, Year 12 Higher School Certificate Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q13(c)

(i) Quotient rule for the first term:

\[\begin{aligned}\dfrac{d}{dx}\left(\dfrac{2x}{4+x^2}\right)+\dfrac{d}{dx}\tan^{-1}\dfrac{x}{2}&=\dfrac{2(4+x^2)-2x\cdot2x}{(4+x^2)^2}+\dfrac{2}{4+x^2}\\&=\dfrac{8-2x^2}{(4+x^2)^2}+\dfrac{2(4+x^2)}{(4+x^2)^2}=\dfrac{16}{(4+x^2)^2}\end{aligned}\]

(ii)

\[\int_0^{2\sqrt3}\dfrac{dx}{(4+x^2)^2}=\tfrac1{16}\Big[\dfrac{2x}{4+x^2}+\tan^{-1}\dfrac{x}{2}\Big]_0^{2\sqrt3}=\tfrac1{16}\left(\dfrac{4\sqrt3}{16}+\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{64}+\dfrac{\pi}{48}\]
