Draw a line through each corner parallel to the two arrowed lines and use alternate angles at each corner in turn. The first segment after the right angle makes \(90^\circ\) with the parallels. At the \(110^\circ\) corner the next segment makes \(110^\circ-90^\circ=20^\circ\). At the \(115^\circ\) corner the next segment makes \(115^\circ-20^\circ=95^\circ\). So the unknown angle is α = 95°.
