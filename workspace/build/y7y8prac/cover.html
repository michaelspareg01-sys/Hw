This pack holds more than one lesson's worth of questions, so choose questions to suit the student. The questions marked ★ make a suggested core set for a 2-hour lesson: one warm-up in each section, then harder questions. Each section runs from easier to harder. Every question is taken word for word from ICE-EM Mathematics 7 (3rd edition) or Cambridge Maths NSW Year 8 (2nd edition), and its source is shown beside it. Times are a guide for a typical Year 7 or Year 8 student.
