Y7 Ch 6 Challenge Q7
The minute hand turns \(6^\circ\) each minute and the hour hand turns \(30^\circ\div60=0.5^\circ\) each minute. After \(t\) minutes the angle between them is \(6t-0.5t=5.5t\) degrees. So \(5.5t=90\), and \(t=\) \(16\tfrac{4}{11}\) minutes.
