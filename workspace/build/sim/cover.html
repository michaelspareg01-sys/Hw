This pack is about linear simultaneous equations: two equations in two unknowns, \(x\) and \(y\), whose graphs are straight lines. A solution of the pair is a point that lies on both lines. The pack solves pairs by drawing graphs, by substitution and by elimination, and then uses them to solve problems. Each lesson runs in the same order: concept → tutor demo → your attempt → worked example to keep for homework. The homework comes in three bands, Easy, Medium and Hard, and each lesson ends with a past-paper set to do under exam conditions. Answers and full past-paper solutions are in the separate Homework Answers booklet.

The lessons follow Cambridge Mathematics Year 11 Advanced (Pender et al., 2019), sections 1G Solving simultaneous equations and 3C Review of linear graphs, using the linear questions only. Lesson 1 also uses the Key ideas of section 7G of Cambridge Maths NSW Stage 4 Year 8 (second edition), and the revision pages use chapters 1, 2 and 7 of the same Year 8 book. The homework also uses six worksheets on simultaneous equations and problem solving. The past-paper sets use Year 9 and Year 10 yearly examinations from schools.
