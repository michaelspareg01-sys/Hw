Year 8 Exercise 6C Q6
a The base angles of an isosceles triangle are equal, so \(a+a+50=180\) (angle sum of a triangle). Then \(2a=130\), so \(a=65\).
b The equal sides meet at the \(100^\circ\) angle, so \(a^\circ\) is a base angle and the other base angle is also \(a^\circ\). Then \(a+a+100=180\) and \(2a=80\), so \(a=40\).
c The equal sides meet at the \(28^\circ\) angle, so \(a+a+28=180\) and \(2a=152\), so \(a=76\).

Year 8 Exercise 8H Q5

In \(\triangle ABC\) and \(\triangle BCD\):
\(\angle ABC=\angle BCD\) (angles in a square are equal)
\(AB=BC\) (sides of a square are equal)
\(BC\) is common.
\(\therefore\triangle ABC\equiv\triangle BCD\) (SAS)
\(\therefore AC=BD\) (matching sides in congruent triangles are equal)
and the diagonals of a square are equal in length.
