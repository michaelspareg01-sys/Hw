In this proof, we construct two isosceles triangles. Let \(ABCD\) be a cyclic trapezium with \(AB\parallel DC\). Since \(ABCD\) is not a rectangle, and its angles add to \(360^\circ\), one of its angles is acute.

so \(\angle B=\alpha\) (corresponding angles, \(AB\parallel DC\)).

and \(\angle CDM=\alpha\) (corresponding angles, \(AB\parallel DC\)).
Hence \(\triangle DMC\) and \(\triangle AMB\) are both isosceles, with \(DM=CM\) and \(AM=BM\), so \(AD=BC\).

Sydney Boys High School, 2021, Year 10 Stage 5.3 Term 4 Yearly Assessment, Mathematics, Part G (e)
i In cyclic quadrilateral \(AXBO\), \(\angle AOB+\angle AXB=180^\circ\) (opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary) and \(\angle AXB=60^\circ\) (\(\triangle AXB\) is equilateral), so \(\angle AOB=180^\circ-60^\circ=120^\circ\). In the same way, in \(BYCO\), \(\angle BOC=120^\circ\). Then \(\angle AOC=360^\circ-120^\circ-120^\circ=120^\circ\) (angles at a point).
ii In quadrilateral \(AOCZ\), \(\angle AZC=60^\circ\) (angle in equilateral \(\triangle AZC\)), so \(\angle AOC+\angle AZC=120^\circ+60^\circ=180^\circ\). Therefore \(AOCZ\) is cyclic (opposite angles are supplementary).
iii Construct the chord \(OB\) with midpoint \(M\). In circle \(\mathcal{C}_1\), \(\angle PMO=90^\circ\) (the line from the centre of a circle that bisects a chord is perpendicular to the chord). By the same logic in \(\mathcal{C}_2\), \(\angle QMO=90^\circ\). Since \(\angle PMO+\angle QMO=180^\circ\), \(PMQ\) is a straight line.
Construct the chord \(OA\) with midpoint \(N\). By the same logic, in \(\mathcal{C}_1\) and in circle \(AOCZ\), \(\angle PNO=90^\circ\) and \(PNR\) is a straight line.
In quadrilateral \(PMON\): \(\angle NPM+\angle PMO+\angle MON+\angle ONP=360^\circ\) (angle sum of a quadrilateral), so \(\angle NPM+90^\circ+120^\circ+90^\circ=360^\circ\) (\(\angle MON=\angle AOB\), found in (i)), and \(\angle RPQ=\angle NPM=60^\circ\).
By the same logic, using the chord \(OC\), \(\angle PQR=60^\circ\). Therefore \(\triangle PQR\) is equilateral (all its angles are \(60^\circ\)).
