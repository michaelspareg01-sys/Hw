This pack covers the circle geometry of the NSW syllabus (MA5-CIR-P-01): the terminology, the chord and angle properties, tangents and secants, and their proofs. Each lesson runs in the same order: concept → tutor demo → your attempt → worked example to keep for homework. The homework comes in three bands, Easy, Medium and Hard, and each lesson ends with a past-paper set to do under exam conditions. Answers and full past-paper solutions are in the separate Homework Answers booklet.

The lessons follow the AMSI TIMES module Circle Geometry (Years 9–10, Module 26). The homework also uses the Maths Pathway worksheets and Year 10 yearly examinations from NSW schools.
