Past-Paper Homework - Worked Solutions
PP 1Normanhurst Boys' High School, 2022, Mathematics Extension 1 Assessment Task 4 (Trial Examination), Q9

\(R\cos(x-\theta)=R\cos x\cos\theta+R\sin x\sin\theta\). Equating coefficients, \(R\cos\theta=3\) and \(R\sin\theta=2\), so \(\tan\theta=\dfrac{R\sin\theta}{R\cos\theta}=\dfrac23\).

Answer (C). (A) and (B) have the wrong sign, and (D) is \(\dfrac{\cos}{\sin}\) instead of \(\dfrac{\sin}{\cos}\).

PP 2Sydney Grammar School, 2021, Trial Examination, Form VI Mathematics Extension 1, Q12(e)

(i) \(R\cos(x-\alpha)=R\cos x\cos\alpha+R\sin x\sin\alpha\), so \(R\cos\alpha=3\), \(R\sin\alpha=2\).

\(R=\sqrt{9+4}=\sqrt{13}\), and \(\tan\alpha=\tfrac23\) with \(\alpha\) acute, so \(\alpha\approx33.69^\circ\):

\[3\cos x+2\sin x=\sqrt{13}\cos(x-33.69^\circ)\]

(ii) \(\sqrt{13}\cos(x-\alpha)=\sqrt{13}\), so \(\cos(x-\alpha)=1\), where \(-\alpha\le x-\alpha\le360^\circ-\alpha\). The only solution is \(x-\alpha=0\), so \(x=\alpha\approx33.69^\circ\).

PP 3Baulkham Hills High School, 2023, Higher School Certificate Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q12(c)

(i) \(A\cos\alpha=6\), \(A\sin\alpha=8\), so \(A=10\) and \(\tan\alpha=\tfrac43\): \(\alpha=\tan^{-1}\tfrac43\approx0.927\).

\[6\cos x+8\sin x=10\cos\left(x-\tan^{-1}\tfrac43\right)\]

(ii) \(\cos(x-\alpha)=\tfrac12\), where \(-0.927\le x-\alpha\le 2\pi-0.927\approx5.356\).

\(x-\alpha=\tfrac{\pi}{3}\) or \(\tfrac{5\pi}{3}\) (\(-\tfrac{\pi}{3}\approx-1.047\) is outside the range).

\(x=\alpha+\tfrac{\pi}{3}\approx1.974\)   or   \(x=\alpha+\tfrac{5\pi}{3}\approx6.163\).

PP 4North Sydney Boys High School, 2023, Year 12 Mathematics Extension 1 Assessment Task 3, Q11(b)

(i) \(R\sin(2x+\alpha)=R\sin2x\cos\alpha+R\cos2x\sin\alpha\), so \(R\cos\alpha=1\), \(R\sin\alpha=\sqrt3\). Then \(R=2\) and \(\alpha=\tfrac{\pi}{3}\):

\[\sin2x+\sqrt3\cos2x=2\sin\left(2x+\tfrac{\pi}{3}\right)\]

(ii) The greatest value is \(2\), when \(\sin\left(2x+\tfrac{\pi}{3}\right)=1\). With \(\tfrac{\pi}{3}\le 2x+\tfrac{\pi}{3}\le4\pi+\tfrac{\pi}{3}\): \(2x+\tfrac{\pi}{3}=\tfrac{\pi}{2}\) or \(\tfrac{5\pi}{2}\), so \(x=\tfrac{\pi}{12}\) or \(\tfrac{13\pi}{12}\).

(iii) \(\sin\left(2x+\tfrac{\pi}{3}\right)=\tfrac12\), with \(\tfrac{\pi}{3}\le2x+\tfrac{\pi}{3}\le\tfrac{13\pi}{3}\):

\[2x+\tfrac{\pi}{3}=\tfrac{5\pi}{6},\ \tfrac{13\pi}{6},\ \tfrac{17\pi}{6},\ \tfrac{25\pi}{6}\qquad(\tfrac{\pi}{6}\text{ is too small})\]

So \(x=\tfrac{\pi}{4},\ \tfrac{11\pi}{12},\ \tfrac{5\pi}{4},\ \tfrac{23\pi}{12}\).
