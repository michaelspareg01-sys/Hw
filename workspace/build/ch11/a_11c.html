PP 1Fort Street High School, 2020, Trial Higher School Certificate Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q3
\[\tan\theta\sin\theta=\dfrac{2t}{1-t^2}\times\dfrac{2t}{1+t^2}=\dfrac{4t^2}{(1-t^2)(1+t^2)}=\dfrac{4t^2}{1-t^4}\]

Answer A. C uses \(1+t^4\), which is not \((1-t^2)(1+t^2)\); B and D have the wrong numerator.

PP 2Hornsby Girls High School, 2023, Year 12 Higher School Certificate Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q11(c)
\[\begin{aligned} \dfrac{2(1-t^2)}{1+t^2}+\dfrac{6t}{1+t^2}&=-2,\quad\theta\ne\pi\\ 2-2t^2+6t&=-2-2t^2\\ 6t&=-4,\qquad t=-\tfrac23 \end{aligned}\]

With \(0\le\tfrac{\theta}{2}\le\pi\): \(\tan\tfrac{\theta}{2}=-\tfrac23\) gives \(\tfrac{\theta}{2}=\pi-\tan^{-1}\tfrac23\approx2.554\), so \(\theta\approx5.11\).

The \(t^2\) terms cancelled (and the coefficient of \(\cos\theta\), 2, is the opposite of the constant \(-2\)), so check \(\theta=\pi\): \(2(-1)+3(0)=-2\). ✓

So \(\theta=\pi\) or \(\theta\approx5.11\).

PP 3Kinross Wolaroi School, 2023, Year 12 Mathematics Extension 1 Trial HSC Examination, Q12(b)

Multiplying by 2: \(5\sin x+2\cos x=4\). With \(t=\tan\tfrac{x}{2}\):

\[\begin{aligned} 10t+2(1-t^2)&=4(1+t^2)\\ 6t^2-10t+2&=0\\ 3t^2-5t+1&=0,\qquad t=\dfrac{5\pm\sqrt{13}}{6} \end{aligned}\]

With \(0^\circ\le\tfrac{x}{2}\le90^\circ\): \(\tfrac{x}{2}\approx55.117^\circ\) or \(13.084^\circ\), so \(x\approx110^\circ14'\) or \(26^\circ10'\).

Check \(x=180^\circ\): \(\tfrac52(0)+(-1)=-1\ne2\), so it is not a solution.

PP 4Normanhurst Boys' High School, 2021, Mathematics Extension 1 (Year 12 Course) Assessment Task 4 (Trial Examination), Q11(c)

With \(t=\tan\theta\): \(\sin2\theta=\dfrac{2t}{1+t^2}\), \(\cos2\theta=\dfrac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}\).

\[\begin{aligned} 4t&=2(1+t^2)+4(1-t^2)\\ 2t^2+4t-6&=0\\ (t+3)(t-1)&=0 \end{aligned}\]

\(\tan\theta=1\): \(\theta=45^\circ, 225^\circ\).   \(\tan\theta=-3\): \(\theta\approx108^\circ26', 288^\circ26'\).

Check \(\theta=90^\circ, 270^\circ\): LHS \(=2\sin180^\circ=0\), RHS \(=2+4(-1)=-2\), so neither is a solution.

So \(\theta=45^\circ,\ 108^\circ26',\ 225^\circ,\ 288^\circ26'\).

PP 5Ascham School, 2023, Year 12 Mathematics Extension 1 Trial Examination, Q14(a)
\[\begin{aligned} \dfrac{6t}{1+t^2}-\dfrac{5(1-t^2)}{1+t^2}&=5\\ 6t-5+5t^2&=5+5t^2\\ 6t&=10,\qquad\tan x=\tfrac53 \end{aligned}\]

\(x=\tan^{-1}\tfrac53\approx1.030\) or \(x\approx1.030+\pi\approx4.172\).

So \(x=1.03,\ \tfrac{\pi}{2},\ 4.17,\ \tfrac{3\pi}{2}\).

PP 6Abbotsleigh, 2020, Higher School Certificate Assessment 4 Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q11(a)

Let \(t=\tan2\theta\), so \(\sin4\theta=\dfrac{2t}{1+t^2}\) and \(\cos4\theta=\dfrac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}\), with \(0\le2\theta\le2\pi\).

\[\begin{aligned} 2t+\sqrt3(1-t^2)&=0\\ \sqrt3\,t^2-2t-\sqrt3&=0\\ t=\dfrac{2\pm\sqrt{4+12}}{2\sqrt3}&=\sqrt3\ \text{or}\ -\tfrac{1}{\sqrt3} \end{aligned}\]

\(\tan2\theta=\sqrt3\): \(2\theta=\tfrac{\pi}{3},\tfrac{4\pi}{3}\), so \(\theta=\tfrac{\pi}{6},\tfrac{2\pi}{3}\).

\(\tan2\theta=-\tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}\): \(2\theta=\tfrac{5\pi}{6},\tfrac{11\pi}{6}\), so \(\theta=\tfrac{5\pi}{12},\tfrac{11\pi}{12}\).

Check \(2\theta=\tfrac{\pi}{2},\tfrac{3\pi}{2}\) (\(4\theta=\pi,3\pi\)): \(0+\sqrt3(-1)\ne0\), so no extra solutions.

So \(\theta=\tfrac{\pi}{6},\ \tfrac{5\pi}{12},\ \tfrac{2\pi}{3},\ \tfrac{11\pi}{12}\).

PP 7Normanhurst Boys' High School, 2023, Year 12 Mathematics Extension 1 Course Assessment Task 4 (Trial Examination), Q12(b)

With \(t=\tan A\): \(\cos2A=\dfrac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}\), \(\sin2A=\dfrac{2t}{1+t^2}\).

\[\begin{aligned} p(1-t^2)-2t&=1+t^2\\ (p+1)t^2+2t-(p-1)&=0\\ \big((p+1)t-(p-1)\big)(t+1)&=0 \end{aligned}\]

(Check by expanding: \((p+1)t^2+(p+1)t-(p-1)t-(p-1)=(p+1)t^2+2t-(p-1)\).)

So \(t=-1\) or \(t=\dfrac{p-1}{p+1}\). But \(0\le A\le\tfrac{\pi}{2}\) means \(\tan A\ge0\), so \(t=-1\) is rejected, and

\[t=\dfrac{p-1}{p+1}.\]

(This needs \(p\ne-1\); at \(A=\tfrac{\pi}{2}\), where \(t\) is undefined, the equation reads \(-p=1\).)
