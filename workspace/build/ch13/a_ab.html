Substituting \(y=e^{px}\): \(p^2e^{px}-pe^{px}-12e^{px}=0\). Since \(e^{px}\ne0\), \(p^2-p-12=0\), \((p-4)(p+3)=0\), so \(p=4\) or \(p=-3\). Answer (B).

PP 4Gosford High School, 2022, Trial Higher School Certificate Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q11(a)

\(y'=3Ae^{3x}-2Be^{-2x}\) and \(y''=9Ae^{3x}+4Be^{-2x}\).

\[y''-y'-6y=(9-3-6)Ae^{3x}+(4+2-6)Be^{-2x}=0\]

PP 5Sydney Boys High School, 2020, Year 12 Term 3 Trial HSC, Mathematics Extension 1, Q13(a)

(i) Write \(u=e^{-\frac t3}\). Then \(K=\dfrac{21000}{7+3u}\) and \(\dfrac{dK}{dt}=\dfrac{21000\times3u\times\frac13}{(7+3u)^2}=\dfrac{21000u}{(7+3u)^2}\).

Also \(1-\dfrac{K}{3000}=1-\dfrac{7}{7+3u}=\dfrac{3u}{7+3u}\), so \(\dfrac13\left(1-\dfrac{K}{3000}\right)K=\dfrac13\times\dfrac{3u}{7+3u}\times\dfrac{21000}{7+3u}=\dfrac{21000u}{(7+3u)^2}\). ✓

(ii) At \(t=0\): \(K=\dfrac{21000}{10}=2100\) kangaroos.

(iii) As \(t\to\infty\), \(e^{-\frac t3}\to0\), so \(K\to\dfrac{21000}{7}=3000\) kangaroos.

(iv) \(\dfrac{1}{K}\dfrac{dK}{dt}=\dfrac13\left(1-\dfrac{2100}{3000}\right)=\dfrac13\times0.3=0.1\), that is, 10% per year.

PP 6Blacktown Boys' High School, 2020, HSC Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q13(b)

(i) \(\dfrac{dF}{dn}=-275ke^{-k(n-1)}=-k(F-F_0)\), since \(F-F_0=275e^{-k(n-1)}\).

(ii) \(n=1\): \(350=275+F_0\), so \(F_0=75\).

(iii) \(80=275e^{-199k}+75\), so \(e^{-199k}=\dfrac{5}{275}=\dfrac{1}{55}\) and \(k=\dfrac{\ln55}{199}\).

(iv) As \(n\to\infty\), \(e^{-k(n-1)}\to0\) (as \(k>0\)), so \(F\to75\). The endurance limit is 75.
