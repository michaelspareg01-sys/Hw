Past-Paper Homework - Worked Solutions
PP 1Abbotsleigh, 2020, Higher School Certificate Assessment 4 Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q13(b)

(i) \(R=21\). \(\dfrac{dT}{dt}=-75ke^{-kt}=-k(T-21)\), since \(T-21=75e^{-kt}\).

(ii) \(t=5\): \(87=21+75e^{-5k}\), so \(e^{-5k}=0.88\) and \(k=-\tfrac15\ln0.88\approx0.02557\).

\(T=70\): \(e^{-kt}=\tfrac{49}{75}\), so \(t=\dfrac{\ln\frac{75}{49}}{k}\approx16.6\) minutes after leaving the stove.

So Miriam waits about \(16.6-5\approx11.6\) more minutes (about 12 minutes).

PP 2Sydney Grammar School, 2023, Trial Examination, Form VI Mathematics Extension 1, Q12(b)

(i) \(\dfrac{dP}{dt}=17ke^{-kt}=k(25-P)\), since \(25-P=17e^{-kt}\).

(ii) \(8.072=25-17e^{-k}\), so \(e^{-k}=\dfrac{16.928}{17}\) and \(k=-\ln\dfrac{16.928}{17}\approx0.00424\).

(iii) \(20=25-17e^{-kt}\), so \(e^{-kt}=\tfrac{5}{17}\) and \(t=\dfrac{\ln3.4}{k}\approx289\) years (using \(k=0.00424\); the unrounded \(k\) gives 288).

PP 3Killara High School, 2023, Year 12 Mathematics Extension 1 Trial Examination, Q14(a)

(i) Calories used per day \(=850+15w\). The surplus is \(1600-850-15w=750-15w\) calories per day, and each kilogram is 10 000 calories, so \(\dfrac{dw}{dt}=\dfrac{750-15w}{10\,000}\).

(ii) \(\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dw}{750-15w}=\int\dfrac{dt}{10\,000}\), so \(-\tfrac1{15}\ln|750-15w|=\dfrac{t}{10\,000}+C\), giving \(|750-15w|=Ae^{-0.0015t}\).

At \(t=0\), \(w=80\): \(750-1200=-450\), so \(15w-750=450e^{-0.0015t}\), and \(w=50+30e^{-0.0015t}\).

(iii) Yes. As \(t\to\infty\), \(w\to50\) kg.

PP 4James Ruse Agricultural High School, 2020, Year 12 Trial HSC Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q13(b)

(i) The volume increases by \(3-2=1\) L/min, so the volume is \(10+t\) L and the concentration is \(\dfrac{Q}{10+t}\) kg/L. Salt leaves at \(2\times\dfrac{Q}{10+t}\) kg/min and none enters, so \(\dfrac{dQ}{dt}=-\dfrac{2Q}{10+t}\).

(ii) \(\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dQ}{Q}=-\int\dfrac{2\,dt}{10+t}\), so \(\ln Q=-2\ln(10+t)+C\) and \(Q=\dfrac{A}{(10+t)^2}\).

\(Q(0)=20\): \(A=2000\). Then \(Q(5)=\dfrac{2000}{225}\approx8.89\) kg.

PP 5Ascham School, 2023, Year 12 Mathematics Extension 1 Trial Examination, Q13(d)

\(\displaystyle\int\dfrac{10}{N(10-N)}\,dN=\int\dfrac{10}{200}\,dt\), so \(\ln\left|\dfrac{N}{10-N}\right|=\dfrac{t}{20}+C\).

At \(t=0\), \(N=15\): \(\left|\dfrac{15}{-5}\right|=3\), so \(C=\ln3\). Since \(N>10\) throughout, \(\dfrac{N}{N-10}=3e^{\frac t{20}}\), and

\[N=\dfrac{30e^{\frac t{20}}}{3e^{\frac t{20}}-1}=\dfrac{30}{3-e^{-\frac t{20}}}.\]

At \(t=6\): \(N=\dfrac{30}{3-e^{-0.3}}\approx13.3\), so about 13 million fish remain.
