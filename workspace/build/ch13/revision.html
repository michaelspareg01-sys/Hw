Logarithms and exponentials
\[\int\dfrac{1}{x}\,dx=\log|x|+C\qquad \int\dfrac{f'(x)}{f(x)}\,dx=\log|f(x)|+C\qquad \int e^{ax+b}\,dx=\tfrac1a e^{ax+b}+C\] \[\log a-\log b=\log\dfrac ab\qquad e^{\log x}=x\qquad \log|y|=u\ \Longleftrightarrow\ |y|=e^{u}\]
Inverse trigonometric integrals (Chapter 12)
\[\int\dfrac{1}{a^2+x^2}\,dx=\dfrac1a\tan^{-1}\dfrac{x}{a}+C\qquad \int\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{a^2-x^2}}\,dx=\sin^{-1}\dfrac{x}{a}+C\]
Differentiating expressions involving y

By the chain rule, \(\dfrac{d}{dx}\big(f(y)\big)=f'(y)\,\dfrac{dy}{dx}\). For example \(\dfrac{d}{dx}(y^2)=2yy'\) and, by the product rule, \(\dfrac{d}{dx}(xy)=y+xy'\).
