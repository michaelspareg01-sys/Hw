Past-Paper Homework - Worked Solutions
PP 1Blacktown Boys' High School, 2022, Year 11 Yearly Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q3

With \(A=4x\) and \(B=x\): \(2\sin4x\cos x=\sin5x+\sin3x\), so \(\sin5x+\sin3x=2\sin4x\cos x\). Answer A.

PP 2St George Girls High School, 2020, Trial HSC Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q8

\(\cos5x\cos2x=\tfrac12\big(\cos7x+\cos3x\big)\) and \(\sin6x\sin3x=\tfrac12\big(\cos3x-\cos9x\big)\).

So the expression is \(\tfrac12\big(\cos7x+\cos9x\big)=\tfrac12\big(\cos(8x-x)+\cos(8x+x)\big)=\) \(\cos8x\cos x\). Answer (D).

PP 3Ascham School, 2023, Year 12 Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q7

\(2\sin3x\sin5x=\cos(3x-5x)-\cos(3x+5x)=\) \(\cos2x-\cos8x\), because \(\cos(-2x)=\cos2x\). Answer C.

PP 4Gosford High School, 2023, Trial HSC Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q10

With \(A=3\alpha\) and \(B=2\alpha\): \(2\cos3\alpha\sin2\alpha=\sin5\alpha-\sin\alpha\), so \(\cos3\alpha\sin2\alpha=\) \(\dfrac12\big(\sin5\alpha-\sin\alpha\big)\). Answer B.

PP 5Sydney Girls High School, 2021, (Online) Year 11 Assessment Task 3, Mathematics Extension 1, Q1(e)

\(\sin255^\circ\cos15^\circ=\tfrac12\big(\sin270^\circ+\sin240^\circ\big)=\tfrac12\Big(-1-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\Big)=\) \(-\dfrac{2+\sqrt3}{4}\).

PP 6Penrith Selective High School, 2024, Year 11 Assessment Task 3, Mathematics Extension 1, Q14(e)

\(2\sin2x\sin x=\cos x-\cos3x\), so

\(\text{LHS}=2\sin3x\big(\cos x-\cos3x\big)=2\sin3x\cos x-2\sin3x\cos3x\)

\(=\big(\sin4x+\sin2x\big)-\sin6x=\text{RHS}\).

PP 7Hornsby Girls High School, 2021, Mathematics Extension 1, Year 11 Assessment 3 Term 3 2021, Q8(b)

(i) \(\text{RHS}=2\sin2x\cos x=\sin(2x+x)+\sin(2x-x)=\sin3x+\sin x=\text{LHS}\).

(ii) By (i), \(2\sin2x\cos x+\sin2x=0\), so \(\sin2x\,(2\cos x+1)=0\).

\(\sin2x=0\) with \(0\le2x\le4\pi\) gives \(x=0,\ \dfrac\pi2,\ \pi,\ \dfrac{3\pi}2,\ 2\pi\); \(\cos x=-\dfrac12\) gives \(x=\dfrac{2\pi}3,\ \dfrac{4\pi}3\).

\(x=0,\ \dfrac\pi2,\ \dfrac{2\pi}3,\ \pi,\ \dfrac{4\pi}3,\ \dfrac{3\pi}2,\ 2\pi\)

PP 8Blacktown Boys' High School, 2022, HSC Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q13(c)

(i) \(2\sin3\theta\cos2\theta=\sin5\theta+\sin\theta\) and \(2\cos3\theta\cos2\theta=\cos5\theta+\cos\theta\). So

\(\text{LHS}=\dfrac{2\sin3\theta\cos2\theta+\sin3\theta}{2\cos3\theta\cos2\theta+\cos3\theta}=\dfrac{\sin3\theta\,(2\cos2\theta+1)}{\cos3\theta\,(2\cos2\theta+1)}=\tan3\theta=\text{RHS}\).

(ii) \(\tan3\theta=1\) with \(0\le3\theta\le6\pi\): \(3\theta=\dfrac\pi4,\ \dfrac{5\pi}4,\ \dfrac{9\pi}4,\ \dfrac{13\pi}4,\ \dfrac{17\pi}4,\ \dfrac{21\pi}4\). None of these makes \(2\cos2\theta+1=0\).

\(\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{12},\ \dfrac{5\pi}{12},\ \dfrac{3\pi}{4},\ \dfrac{13\pi}{12},\ \dfrac{17\pi}{12},\ \dfrac{7\pi}{4}\)

PP 9Penrith Selective High School, 2023, Preliminary Assessment Task 3, Extension 1 Mathematics, Q14(c)

With \(A=5x\) and \(B=2x\): \(\cos7x-\cos3x=-2\sin5x\sin2x\). So \(-2\sin5x\sin2x=\sin5x\), that is, \(\sin5x\,(2\sin2x+1)=0\).

\(\sin5x=0\) with \(0\le5x\le5\pi\) gives \(x=0,\ \dfrac\pi5,\ \dfrac{2\pi}5,\ \dfrac{3\pi}5,\ \dfrac{4\pi}5,\ \pi\). \(\sin2x=-\dfrac12\) with \(0\le2x\le2\pi\) gives \(2x=\dfrac{7\pi}6,\ \dfrac{11\pi}6\), so \(x=\dfrac{7\pi}{12},\ \dfrac{11\pi}{12}\).

\(x=0,\ \dfrac\pi5,\ \dfrac{2\pi}5,\ \dfrac{7\pi}{12},\ \dfrac{3\pi}5,\ \dfrac{4\pi}5,\ \dfrac{11\pi}{12},\ \pi\)
