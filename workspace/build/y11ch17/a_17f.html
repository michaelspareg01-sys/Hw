Past-Paper Homework - Worked Solutions
PP 1North Sydney Girls High School, 2023, HSC Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q4

\(\dfrac{\sin\theta+1}{\cos\theta}=\dfrac{2t+1+t^2}{1+t^2}\times\dfrac{1+t^2}{1-t^2}=\dfrac{(1+t)^2}{(1-t)(1+t)}=\) \(\dfrac{1+t}{1-t}\). Answer B.

PP 2North Sydney Boys High School, 2023, Mathematics Extension 1, Year 12, Assessment Task 3, Term 2 2023, Q8

\(4\sin\theta+3\cos\theta+5=\dfrac{8t+3-3t^2+5+5t^2}{1+t^2}=\dfrac{2t^2+8t+8}{1+t^2}=\) \(\dfrac{2(t+2)^2}{1+t^2}\). Answer C.

PP 3Caringbah High School, 2020, Preliminary Task 3, Mathematics Extension 1, Q14(b)

(i) \(\operatorname{cosec}\theta\,(\cos\theta-1)=\dfrac{1+t^2}{2t}\times\dfrac{1-t^2-1-t^2}{1+t^2}=\dfrac{1+t^2}{2t}\times\dfrac{-2t^2}{1+t^2}=\) \(-t\).

(ii) \(-t=-1\), so \(\tan\dfrac\theta2=1\). With \(0^\circ<\dfrac\theta2<90^\circ\) (\(\operatorname{cosec}\theta\) is undefined at \(0^\circ\) and \(180^\circ\)), \(\dfrac\theta2=45^\circ\), so \(\theta=90^\circ\).

PP 4Gosford High School, 2023, Trial HSC Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q11(b)

Let \(t=\tan\dfrac x2\). \(\text{LHS}=\dfrac{1-t^2}{2t}+t=\dfrac{1-t^2+2t^2}{2t}=\dfrac{1+t^2}{2t}=\operatorname{cosec}x=\text{RHS}\).

PP 5SCEGGS Darlinghurst, 2021, Year 11 Task 3, Mathematics Extension 1, Q7(c)

Multiply the top and bottom by \(1+t^2\):

\(\text{LHS}=\dfrac{2t+1+t^2-1+t^2}{2t-1-t^2+1-t^2}=\dfrac{2t^2+2t}{2t-2t^2}=\dfrac{2t(t+1)}{2t(1-t)}=\dfrac{1+t}{1-t}=\dfrac{1+\tan\big(\frac\theta2\big)}{1-\tan\big(\frac\theta2\big)}=\text{RHS}\).

PP 6Girraween High School, 2023, Year 12 Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q13(a)

\(1+\cos x+\sin x=\dfrac{1+t^2+1-t^2+2t}{1+t^2}=\dfrac{2(1+t)}{1+t^2}\) and \(1-\cos x+\sin x=\dfrac{1+t^2-1+t^2+2t}{1+t^2}=\dfrac{2t(1+t)}{1+t^2}\).

Hence \(\dfrac{1+\cos x+\sin x}{1-\cos x+\sin x}=\dfrac{2(1+t)}{2t(1+t)}=\dfrac1t=\cot\dfrac x2\).

PP 7Hornsby Girls High School, 2020, Mathematics Extension 1, Year 12 Higher School Certificate Trial Examination Term 3 2020, Q11(c)

\(\dfrac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}-\dfrac{2t}{1+t^2}=-1\), so \(1-t^2-2t=-1-t^2\), \(2t=2\) and \(t=1\). With \(0\le\dfrac x2\le\pi\), \(\dfrac x2=\dfrac\pi4\), so \(x=\dfrac\pi2\).

Also, when \(x=\pi\): \(\cos\pi-\sin\pi=-1-0=-1\). Hence \(x=\dfrac\pi2\text{ or }\pi\).

PP 8Knox Grammar School, 2023, Year 11 Yearly Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q12(a)

\(\dfrac{2t}{1+t^2}+\sqrt3\times\dfrac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}=1\), so \(2t+\sqrt3-\sqrt3t^2=1+t^2\), that is, \(\big(1+\sqrt3\big)t^2-2t+1-\sqrt3=0\).

\(t=\dfrac{2\pm\sqrt{4-4\big(1+\sqrt3\big)\big(1-\sqrt3\big)}}{2\big(1+\sqrt3\big)}=\dfrac{2\pm2\sqrt3}{2\big(1+\sqrt3\big)}\), so \(t=1\) or \(t=\dfrac{1-\sqrt3}{1+\sqrt3}=\sqrt3-2\).

With \(0\le\dfrac\theta2\le\pi\): \(t=1\) gives \(\dfrac\theta2=\dfrac\pi4\), and \(t=\sqrt3-2=-\tan\dfrac{\pi}{12}\) gives \(\dfrac\theta2=\pi-\dfrac\pi{12}=\dfrac{11\pi}{12}\). Test \(\theta=\pi\): \(\sin\pi+\sqrt3\cos\pi=-\sqrt3\ne1\).

Hence \(\theta=\dfrac\pi2\text{ or }\dfrac{11\pi}{6}\).

PP 9Sydney Grammar School, 2022, Trial Examination, Form VI Mathematics Extension 1, Q11(d)

\(\dfrac{2t}{1+t^2}+\dfrac{2\big(1-t^2\big)}{1+t^2}=2\), so \(2t+2-2t^2=2+2t^2\), that is, \(4t^2-2t=0\) and \(2t(2t-1)=0\). So \(t=0\) or \(t=\dfrac12\).

With \(0^\circ\le\dfrac\theta2\le180^\circ\): \(t=0\) gives \(\dfrac\theta2=0^\circ\) or \(180^\circ\), so \(\theta=0^\circ\) or \(360^\circ\); \(t=\dfrac12\) gives \(\dfrac\theta2\doteqdot26.565^\circ\), so \(\theta\doteqdot53^\circ\).

Hence \(\theta=0^\circ,\ 53^\circ,\ 360^\circ\).
