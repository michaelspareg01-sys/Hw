Past-Paper Homework - Worked Solutions
PP 1Sydney Grammar School, 2020, Trial Examination, Form VI Mathematics Extension 1, Q5

\(1-\cos2x=1-\big(1-2\sin^2x\big)=\) \(2\sin^2x\). Answer (C).

PP 2Cherrybrook Technology High School, 2020, Year 12 AP4, Mathematics Extension 1, Q7

\(\sin x=\dfrac{2\sqrt3}{4}=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\) and \(x\) is obtuse, so \(\cos x=-\dfrac12\). Then \(\sin2x=2\times\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\times\Big(-\dfrac12\Big)=\) \(-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\). Answer B.

PP 3Blacktown Boys' High School, 2024, HSC Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q5

\(\sin x=0.28=\dfrac{7}{25}\), and \(x\) is in the second quadrant, so \(\cos x=-\dfrac{24}{25}\) (a 7, 24, 25 triangle) and \(\tan x=-\dfrac{7}{24}\). Then \(\tan2x=\dfrac{2\big(-\frac{7}{24}\big)}{1-\frac{49}{576}}=-\dfrac{7}{12}\times\dfrac{576}{527}=\) \(-\dfrac{336}{527}\). Answer D.

PP 4The King's School, 2021, Year 12 Alternative Higher School Certificate Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q11(c)

\(\cos x<0\) and \(\sin x>0\), so \(x\) is in the second quadrant, and \(\sin x=\sqrt{1-\frac19}=\dfrac{2\sqrt2}{3}\). Hence \(\sin2x=2\times\dfrac{2\sqrt2}{3}\times\Big(-\dfrac13\Big)=\) \(-\dfrac{4\sqrt2}{9}\).

PP 5North Sydney Girls High School, 2020, HSC Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q13(a)

Let \(\alpha=\cos^{-1}\dfrac{12}{13}\). Then \(\alpha\) is acute and \(\tan\alpha=\dfrac{5}{12}\) (a 5, 12, 13 triangle). So \(\tan2\alpha=\dfrac{2\times\frac{5}{12}}{1-\frac{25}{144}}=\dfrac56\times\dfrac{144}{119}=\) \(\dfrac{120}{119}\).

PP 6Normanhurst Boys High School, 2020, Mathematics Extension 1, Year 12 Course Assessment Task 4 (Trial Examination), Q11(e)

Let \(A=\sin^{-1}\dfrac23\). Then \(A\) is acute, the adjacent side is \(\sqrt{9-4}=\sqrt5\), and \(\tan A=\dfrac{2}{\sqrt5}\). So \(\tan2A=\dfrac{\frac{4}{\sqrt5}}{1-\frac45}=\dfrac{4}{\sqrt5}\times5=\dfrac{20}{\sqrt5}=\) \(4\sqrt5\).

PP 7Normanhurst Boys High School, 2023, Mathematics Extension 1, Year 12 Course Assessment Task 4 (Trial Examination), Q11(b)

\(\sin A\sin5A+\cos A\cos5A=\cos5A\cos A+\sin5A\sin A\)
\(=\cos(5A-A)=\cos4A\).

Then \(\cos4A=2\cos^22A-1=2\times\dfrac{4}{25}-1=\) \(-\dfrac{17}{25}\).

PP 8Blacktown Boys' High School, 2023, HSC Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q14(a)

(i) \(\dfrac{\sin2x}{1+\cos2x}=\dfrac{2\sin x\cos x}{1+\big(2\cos^2x-1\big)}=\dfrac{2\sin x\cos x}{2\cos^2x}=\dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}=\tan x\).

(ii) With \(x=15^\circ\), \(\tan15^\circ=\dfrac{\sin30^\circ}{1+\cos30^\circ}\), so \(\cot15^\circ=\dfrac{1+\cos30^\circ}{\sin30^\circ}=\dfrac{1+\frac{\sqrt3}{2}}{\frac12}=\) \(2+\sqrt3\).

PP 9Killara High School, 2021, Year 12 Mathematics Extension 1 Trial Examination, Q14(a)

(i) \(\cos3x=\cos(2x+x)=\cos2x\cos x-\sin2x\sin x\)
\(=\big(2\cos^2x-1\big)\cos x-2\sin^2x\cos x\)
\(=2\cos^3x-\cos x-2\big(1-\cos^2x\big)\cos x=4\cos^3x-3\cos x\).

(ii) \(\cos3x=\sin2x\) gives \(\cos x\big(4\cos^2x-3\big)=2\sin x\cos x\). For \(0<x<\dfrac\pi2\), \(\cos x\ne0\), so \(4\big(1-\sin^2x\big)-3=2\sin x\), that is, \(4\sin^2x+2\sin x-1=0\). Then \(\sin x=\dfrac{-2\pm\sqrt{4+16}}{8}=\dfrac{-1\pm\sqrt5}{4}\), and \(\sin x>0\) gives \(\sin x=\dfrac{\sqrt5-1}{4}\).

(iii) By (ii), \(\sin\dfrac{\pi}{10}=\dfrac{\sqrt5-1}{4}=s\). Then \(s^2=\dfrac{6-2\sqrt5}{16}\), so \(\cos^2\dfrac\pi{10}=1-s^2=\dfrac{5+\sqrt5}{8}\). Using \(\sin\dfrac\pi5=2\sin\dfrac\pi{10}\cos\dfrac\pi{10}\),

\[\begin{aligned}\sin\frac\pi5\cos\frac\pi{10}&=2\sin\frac\pi{10}\cos^2\frac\pi{10}\\&=2\times\frac{\sqrt5-1}{4}\times\frac{5+\sqrt5}{8}\\&=\frac{(\sqrt5-1)(5+\sqrt5)}{16}=\frac{4\sqrt5}{16}=\frac{\sqrt5}{4}.\end{aligned}\]
