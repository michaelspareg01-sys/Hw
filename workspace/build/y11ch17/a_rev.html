1
a Let \(y=\dfrac{x+1}{3}\). The inverse is \(x=\dfrac{y+1}{3}\), so \(y+1=3x\) and \(f^{-1}(x)=3x-1\).
b \(f\big(f^{-1}(x)\big)=\dfrac{(3x-1)+1}{3}=\dfrac{3x}{3}=x\) and \(f^{-1}\big(f(x)\big)=3\Big(\dfrac{x+1}{3}\Big)-1=x+1-1=x\).
2
a \(\sin\dfrac{2\pi}{3}=\sin\dfrac\pi3=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\) (second quadrant, sine positive)
b \(\cos\dfrac{5\pi}{4}=-\cos\dfrac\pi4=-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\) (third quadrant)
c \(\tan\Big(-\dfrac\pi6\Big)=-\tan\dfrac\pi6=-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}\) (fourth quadrant)
d \(\sin\dfrac{11\pi}{6}=-\sin\dfrac\pi6=-\dfrac12\) (fourth quadrant)
3
a The related angle is \(\dfrac\pi6\), and sine is negative in quadrants 3 and 4, so \(x=-\dfrac{5\pi}{6}\) or \(x=-\dfrac\pi6\).
b The related angle is \(\dfrac\pi4\), and cosine is positive in quadrants 1 and 4, so \(x=-\dfrac\pi4\) or \(x=\dfrac\pi4\).
c The related angle is \(\dfrac\pi3\), and tangent is negative in quadrants 2 and 4, so \(x=-\dfrac\pi3\) or \(x=\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\).
4
A right triangle with adjacent side 5 and hypotenuse 13 has opposite side \(\sqrt{169-25}=12\). In the second quadrant sine is positive and tangent is negative, so \(\sin\theta=\dfrac{12}{13}\) and \(\tan\theta=-\dfrac{12}{5}\).
5
a \(y=\cos x\) is many-to-one: for example the line \(y=0\) meets the graph at \(x=\pm\dfrac\pi2,\ \pm\dfrac{3\pi}{2},\ \dots\), so it fails the horizontal line test, and the inverse relation is not a function.
b \(-\dfrac\pi2\le x\le\dfrac\pi2\). On this interval \(\sin x\) increases from \(-1\) to \(1\), and any larger interval containing 0 repeats a value.
