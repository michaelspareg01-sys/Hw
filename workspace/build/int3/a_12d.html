Past-Paper Homework - Worked Solutions
PP 1Blacktown Boys' High School, 2020, HSC Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q8
\[\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{3}}\tan^2x\sec^2x\,dx=\int_{0}^{\sqrt3}u^2\,du\]

Let \(u=\tan x\).
Then \(du=\sec^2x\,dx\).
When \(x=0\), \(u=0\),
when \(x=\tfrac{\pi}{3}\), \(u=\sqrt3\).

Answer (B). Option (A) keeps the \(x\)-limits on a \(u\)-integral. Option (C) loses the square on \(\tan x\). Option (D) writes \(\sec^2x\) as \(1+u^2\) as well, but \(\sec^2x\,dx\) has already become \(du\).

PP 2Moriah College, 2023, Trial Higher School Certificate Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q10(a)
\[\begin{aligned}\int x\sqrt{x^2+1}\,dx&=\int u^{\frac12}\times\tfrac12\,du\\&=\tfrac12\times\tfrac23u^{\frac32}+C\\&=\tfrac13(x^2+1)^{\frac32}+C\end{aligned}\]

Let \(u=x^2+1\).
Then \(du=2x\,dx\),
and \(x\,dx=\tfrac12\,du\).

PP 3Sydney Grammar School, 2020, Trial Examination, Form VI Mathematics Extension 1, Q11(d)

From \(u=2x-1\), \(\ 2x=u+1\), so \(4x=2(u+1)\).

\[\begin{aligned}\int4x\sqrt{2x-1}\,dx&=\int2(u+1)\sqrt u\times\tfrac12\,du\\&=\int(u+1)\sqrt u\,du\\&=\int\big(u^{\frac32}+u^{\frac12}\big)\,du\\&=\tfrac25u^{\frac52}+\tfrac23u^{\frac32}+C\\&=\tfrac25(2x-1)^{\frac52}+\tfrac23(2x-1)^{\frac32}+C\end{aligned}\]

Let \(u=2x-1\).
Then \(du=2\,dx\),
and \(dx=\tfrac12\,du\).

PP 4Girraween High School, 2021, Trial Higher School Certificate Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q11(c)

By the chain rule, \(\dfrac{d}{dx}\ln3x=\dfrac{3}{3x}=\dfrac1x\).

\[\begin{aligned}\int\frac{dx}{x(\ln3x)^2}&=\int\frac{1}{u^2}\,du\\&=\int u^{-2}\,du\\&=-u^{-1}+C\\&=-\frac{1}{\ln3x}+C\end{aligned}\]

Let \(u=\ln3x\).
Then \(du=\dfrac1x\,dx\).

PP 5Girraween High School, 2023, Year 12 Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q11(b)
\[\begin{aligned}\int_{1}^{6}x\sqrt{6-x}\,dx&=-\int_{5}^{0}(6-u)\sqrt u\,du\\&=\int_{0}^{5}(6-u)\sqrt u\,du\\&=\int_{0}^{5}\big(6u^{\frac12}-u^{\frac32}\big)\,du\\&=\Big[4u^{\frac32}-\tfrac25u^{\frac52}\Big]_{0}^{5}\\&=\big(4\times5\sqrt5-\tfrac25\times25\sqrt5\big)-\big(0-0\big)\\&=20\sqrt5-10\sqrt5\\&=10\sqrt5\end{aligned}\]

Let \(u=6-x\).
Then \(du=-dx\),
and \(x=6-u\).
When \(x=1\), \(u=5\),
when \(x=6\), \(u=0\).

The second line reverses the limits to remove the minus sign. The primitive of \(6u^{\frac12}\) is \(6\times\tfrac23u^{\frac32}=4u^{\frac32}\). Also \(5^{\frac32}=5\sqrt5\) and \(5^{\frac52}=25\sqrt5\).

PP 6Abbotsleigh, 2023, Higher School Certificate Assessment 4 Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q12(a)

After the substitution, use \(\cos^2u=\tfrac12+\tfrac12\cos2u\) (Lesson 12C).

\[\begin{aligned}\int\frac{\cos^2(\ln x)}{x}\,dx&=\int\cos^2u\,du\\&=\int\big(\tfrac12+\tfrac12\cos2u\big)\,du\\&=\tfrac12u+\tfrac14\sin2u+C\\&=\tfrac12\ln x+\tfrac14\sin(2\ln x)+C\end{aligned}\]

Let \(u=\ln x\).
Then \(du=\dfrac1x\,dx\).

PP 7Knox Grammar School, 2023, Higher School Certificate Trial Examination, Mathematics Extension 1, Q11(d)

The derivative of \(\tan x-x\) is \(\sec^2x-1\), and \(\sec^2x-1=\tan^2x\) (Lesson 12C). So \(du=\tan^2x\,dx\).

\[\begin{aligned}\int\tan^2x\sqrt{\tan x-x}\,dx&=\int u^{\frac12}\,du\\&=\tfrac23u^{\frac32}+C\\&=\tfrac23(\tan x-x)^{\frac32}+C\end{aligned}\]

Let \(u=\tan x-x\).
Then \(du=\tan^2x\,dx\).

PP 8Sydney Technical High School, 2021, Extension 1 Mathematics, Trial HSC, Q12(b)

Since \(4e^{2x}=\big(2e^x\big)^2=u^2\), and \(e^x\,dx=\tfrac12\,du\):

\[\begin{aligned}\int_{\ln\left(\frac12\right)}^{\ln\left(\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)}\frac{e^x}{1+4e^{2x}}\,dx&=\tfrac12\int_{1}^{\sqrt3}\frac{du}{1+u^2}\\&=\tfrac12\Big[\tan^{-1}u\Big]_{1}^{\sqrt3}\\&=\tfrac12\big(\tan^{-1}\sqrt3-\tan^{-1}1\big)\\&=\tfrac12\big(\tfrac{\pi}{3}-\tfrac{\pi}{4}\big)\\&=\tfrac12\times\tfrac{\pi}{12}\\&=\tfrac{\pi}{24}\end{aligned}\]

Let \(u=2e^x\).
Then \(du=2e^x\,dx\),
and \(e^x\,dx=\tfrac12\,du\).
When \(x=\ln\tfrac12\), \(u=1\),
when \(x=\ln\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\), \(u=\sqrt3\).

\(u=2e^{\ln\frac12}=2\times\tfrac12=1\) and \(u=2e^{\ln\frac{\sqrt3}{2}}=2\times\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}=\sqrt3\).
