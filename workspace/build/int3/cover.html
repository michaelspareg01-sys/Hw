This is Part 3 of the Year 12 Extension 1 integration course. It follows Chapter 12 of the Year 12 Extension 1 textbook. Each lesson runs in the same order: concept → tutor demo → your attempt → worked example to keep for homework. Then do the textbook homework and finish with the past-paper set under exam conditions. Start with the Revision section: its summary boxes collect the inverse trigonometric functions, the double-angle formulae and the standard integrals from Parts 1 and 2. Answers and full past-paper solutions are in the separate Homework Answers booklet.
