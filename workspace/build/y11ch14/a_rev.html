a \(\dfrac{13}{52}=\dfrac14\)   b \(\dfrac{12}{52}=\dfrac{3}{13}\)   c \(1-\dfrac{3}{13}=\dfrac{10}{13}\)   d There are 26 red cards and 4 kings, and the 2 red kings are in both. So \(P=\dfrac{26+4-2}{52}=\dfrac{28}{52}=\dfrac{7}{13}\).

a The number who play soccer or tennis is \(18+14-6=26\), so \(4\) play neither and \(P=\dfrac{4}{30}=\dfrac{2}{15}\).   b Soccer only is \(18-6=12\) and tennis only is \(14-6=8\), so \(P=\dfrac{12+8}{30}=\dfrac{2}{3}\).
