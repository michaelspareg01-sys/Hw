Tutor demo.
a Let \(u=x^4\), so \(y=e^u\), \(\dfrac{du}{dx}=4x^3\) and \(\dfrac{dy}{du}=e^u\). Then \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{dy}{du}\times\dfrac{du}{dx}=4x^3e^{x^4}\).
b Reversing part a, \(\displaystyle\int4x^3e^{x^4}\,dx=e^{x^4}\), so \(\displaystyle\int_{-1}^14x^3e^{x^4}\,dx=\Big[e^{x^4}\Big]_{-1}^1=e^1-e^1=0\), because \((-1)^4=1\).
c The function \(4x^3e^{x^4}\) is odd, because \(4(-x)^3e^{(-x)^4}=-4x^3e^{x^4}\), and the interval \([-1,1]\) is symmetric in the \(y\)-axis, so the integral is zero.
Your turn.
a Let \(u=2x^2\), so \(y=e^u\), \(\dfrac{du}{dx}=4x\) and \(\dfrac{dy}{du}=e^u\). Then \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{dy}{du}\times\dfrac{du}{dx}=4xe^{2x^2}\).

Tutor demo. Let \(u=1+e^{2x}\). Then \(u'=2e^{2x}\), and with \(n=-3\), \(\int u^{-3}\,\dfrac{du}{dx}\,dx=\dfrac{u^{-2}}{-2}\). \[\begin{aligned}&\int\dfrac{e^{2x}}{(1+e^{2x})^3}\,dx\\&\quad=\tfrac12\int\dfrac{2e^{2x}}{(1+e^{2x})^3}\,dx\\&\quad=\tfrac12\times\big(-\tfrac12\big)\times(1+e^{2x})^{-2}\\&\quad=-\dfrac{1}{4(1+e^{2x})^2}+C\end{aligned}\]
Your turn. Let \(u=1-e^{3x}\). Then \(u'=-3e^{3x}\), and with \(n=-2\), \(\int u^{-2}\,\dfrac{du}{dx}\,dx=\dfrac{u^{-1}}{-1}\). \[\begin{aligned}&\int\dfrac{e^{3x}}{(1-e^{3x})^2}\,dx\\&\quad=-\tfrac13\int\dfrac{-3e^{3x}}{(1-e^{3x})^2}\,dx\\&\quad=-\tfrac13\times(-1)\times(1-e^{3x})^{-1}\\&\quad=\dfrac{1}{3(1-e^{3x})}+C\end{aligned}\]

PP 1Sydney Boys High School, 2020, Year 12 Term 3 Trial HSC, Mathematics Extension 1, Q11(b)

(i) Using the product rule, let \(y=uv\), where \(u=x\) and \(v=e^{-x}\). Then \(u'=1\) and \(v'=-e^{-x}\), so

\[\begin{aligned}\dfrac{dy}{dx}&=u'v+v'u\\&=1\times e^{-x}+(-e^{-x})\times x\\&=e^{-x}-xe^{-x}\\&=e^{-x}(1-x).\end{aligned}\]

(ii) Reversing part (i),

\[\int_0^1\big(e^{-x}-xe^{-x}\big)\,dx=\Big[xe^{-x}\Big]_0^1.\]

Split the left-hand side into two integrals and make the required integral the subject:

\[\begin{aligned}\int_0^1xe^{-x}\,dx&=\int_0^1e^{-x}\,dx-\Big[xe^{-x}\Big]_0^1\\&=\Big[-e^{-x}\Big]_0^1-\Big[xe^{-x}\Big]_0^1\\&=\big(-e^{-1}-(-e^0)\big)-\big(1\times e^{-1}-0\times e^0\big)\\&=-e^{-1}+1-e^{-1}\\&=1-\dfrac2e.\end{aligned}\]
