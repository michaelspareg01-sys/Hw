Source folder: `workspace/pp/Y10/`. Share B is files 21–40 of `ls | sort`. I looked at every page of every file: each page was rendered with PyMuPDF and read visually, and the text layer was used where it exists.

**Scope note:** the brief said the share runs "from North Sydney Girls 2023", but in `ls | sort` file 21 is **North Sydney Girls 2022** (NSG 2023 is file 22). Files 1–20 end at North Sydney Boys 2022, so NSG 2022 would otherwise be covered by nobody. I have included it (rows y10-B-01 to 06). If the other agent also covered it, remove one copy.

**Tag conventions used:** a sine-rule question is tagged TRG-SINE-AMBIG whether or not the ambiguous case arises; the Notes say "ambiguous case" when it does. An area-rule question is tagged TRG-AREA. Cosine-rule, bearings and 3D questions with non-right-angled triangles are tagged TRG-OTHER. Plain right-angled SOHCAHTOA parts are skipped. Page numbers are 1-based PDF page indices. MC = multiple choice. Rows marked DOUBTFUL have a solution that looks wrong or a flawed or removed question.
