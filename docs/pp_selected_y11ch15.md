## 15B
Method for every item: expand (x + y)^n directly with a row of Pascal's triangle (Box 2, Examples 6-8). Terms with negative powers at n <= 12 follow Example 7 (write each term as a power of x; the power changes by a fixed step from term to term), and products follow Example 8 (expand only as far as needed). No item needs nCr notation or the general term. Rows 10 and 12 come from Ex 15A Q1 (rows 0-12). Order in the lesson is PP 1-9 below. Total 16 marks, 25 min.
