## 15C
Method for every item: the binomial theorem, the term with y^r in (x + y)^n is nCr x^(n-r) y^r, with nCr from the formula (Box 3, Examples 9-10). Negative powers (rows C8, C13, C15, C18) follow Exercise 15C Q15, which uses them: the lesson's Concept 3 teaches the step first (write each term as a power of x; the power changes by a fixed step; count the steps r; the term is nCr a^(n-r) b^r). No T(r+1) general-term notation and no sigma notation (15E). Order in the lesson is PP 1-8 below. Total 18 marks, 25 min.
