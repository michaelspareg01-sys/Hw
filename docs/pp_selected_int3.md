## 12C
Method for every item: sin^2 and cos^2 by sin^2 θ = 1/2 - 1/2 cos 2θ and cos^2 θ = 1/2 + 1/2 cos 2θ (the angle doubles, then divide by its coefficient); products to sums (Concept 4, reviewed from 11A); cos^4 x = (cos^2 x)^2 with the identity used twice (the Concept 2 tip, Ex 12C Q10).
