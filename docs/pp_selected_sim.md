# Year 9–10 Simultaneous Equations pack: questions used

Every question used in the pack is listed here, so that no question is used twice in this pack or in another pack. Past-paper rows give the catalogue ID (`docs/pp_candidates_sim.md`). Re-read this file, every other `docs/pp_selected_*.md` and `docs/PAST_PAPERS_USED.md` before choosing and before committing.

Past-paper labels carry the cover title, because the Year 9 (Form III) and Year 10 (Form IV) papers of the same school and year are different papers: for example `pp_selected_y10trig.md` uses Sydney Grammar School 2020 **Year 10** Q6(c), while sim-17 below is Q6(c)(i) of the **Year 9** paper (Form III).
