## Past-paper candidates: the binomial expansion and Pascal's triangle (Y11 Ext 1 Ch 15, sections 15A to 15D)

All paths are relative to `pp/Ext 1/`. "p" is the PDF page number, not the printed page. I checked every candidate against the rendered page, so the question numbers, marks and wording come from the paper itself. A solution page marked "letter only" gives just the MC answer. Section 15E is skipped. No candidate has a diagram.

Nothing here is on `docs/PAST_PAPERS_USED.md`, `docs/pp_selected_ch10.md` or `docs/pp_selected_y11ch14.md`. The Ch 14 file still had no entries when I last read it, just before committing. I left out all commercial papers (NEAP, PEM, TEC, CSSA, Independent and the BoS zip). I also left out binomial probability and distribution questions.

**A note on tags.** In the textbook, using the general term with negative powers of x (for example "the term independent of x in (2x² − 3/x)⁹") is taught mainly in 15E (Example 11, Exercise 15E Q2 to Q6). Before 15E it appears only with Pascal's triangle for small n (15B Example 7, Exercise 15B Q12 and Q13) and in 15C Q15. Your tag list puts "the term independent of x" in 15C, so I have tagged these items 15C. Each one with negative powers and n ≥ 7 is flagged **neg-power**, so you can tell them apart from the 15C items with positive powers only.

### Weak, doubtful or excluded items
- **Killara 2021 Q1** (`2021/3U Killara.pdf` p2, MC; solution p16): "What is the coefficient of x⁹ in (x + 5)¹⁶?" The options are ¹⁶P₇5⁶, ¹⁶P₉5⁹, ¹⁶C₇5⁶ and ¹⁶C₉5⁹. The correct value ¹⁶C₉·5⁷ is not among them, and the school solution writes "(C) ¹⁶C₉5⁷ (or none)". **Doubtful. Do not use.**
- **JRAHS 2021 mock Q11(c)** (`2021/3U JRAHS.pdf` p6): this is C17 again, from a paper headed "MOCK TRIAL PAPER 2021" that names no school. Use C17 instead.
- **Normanhurst 2023 Q10** (B7) is not a binomial expansion. Use it only as a stretch item.
- **Duplicates:** A5 is the same as A6. A11 and A12 use the same expansion.
- **Factorial identity, not binomial:** Hurlstone 2023 Q12(d) (p8, 3 marks) asks to show (n+1)[n!·n + (n−1)!(2n−1) + (n−2)!(n−1)] = (n+2)!. It belongs to the Ch 14 factorial work. I did not list it above.
- **Binomial probability, excluded:** for example Caringbah 2020 Q11(e), Ruse 2020 Q6, Knox 2022 Q6, Blacktown 2024 Q12(c) and others.
