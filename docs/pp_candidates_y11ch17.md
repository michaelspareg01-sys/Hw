## Past-paper candidates: Year 11 Ext 1 Ch 17 Further trigonometry (17A to 17G), non-calculus parts only

All paths are relative to `workspace/pp/Ext 1/`. "p" is the PDF page, not the printed page. Every row below was checked on the rendered question page (zoom 1.0, scans at 1.5 where needed), so question numbers, marks and wording come from the paper. The solution page is listed where I found it; "no solutions" means the file has none.

Scope: inverse functions and restricted domains (17A), values and definitions of the inverse trigonometric functions (17B), their graphs and transformations (17C), compound angles (17D), double angles, including triple angles and tan 3θ (17E), the t-formulae (17F) and products to sums (17G). Calculus parts (derivatives, integrals, areas, volumes) and auxiliary-angle parts (R sin(x + α), Year 12) are left out. Where only one part of a question is in scope, only that part is listed.

Papers left out: all commercial papers (CSSA, NEAP, PEM, TEC, Independent and the BoS zip), `2020/2020 Western.pdf`, `2021/3U JRAHS.pdf` (2021 mock), `2023/Fort_Street_2023...`, `2024/NSB 3U.pdf` and `2024/Killara 3U.pdf` (garbled duplicate of the Killara 2024 file), as in the y11ch13 spec. The Sydney Tech 2020 files and `2020/2020 SBHS.pdf` are revision sample papers (int3 ruling), `2020/2020 Bauklham Hills.pdf` and `2021/3U NSB.pdf` have no cover page, and `2020/2020 Ruse.pdf` is a marked student script, so none of them is listed.
