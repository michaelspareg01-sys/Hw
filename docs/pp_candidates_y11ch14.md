## Past-paper candidates: Year 11 Ext 1 Chapter 14 Combinatorics (14A–14F)

All paths are relative to `pp/Ext 1/`. "p" is the PDF page number, not the printed page. I checked every candidate against the rendered page, so the question numbers and marks below come from the paper itself. Solution pages were found by eye (many are handwritten scans). Where a file has no solutions I say "none". Nothing here is on `docs/PAST_PAPERS_USED.md`, `docs/pp_selected_ch10.md` or `docs/pp_selected_y11ch15.md` (empty when checked). I left out all commercial papers (NEAP, PEM, TEC, CSSA, Independent and the BoS zip). I also left out anything that needs circular arrangements (14G), the pigeonhole principle (14H), the binomial theorem or the binomial distribution, and induction proofs. The rejected items are listed at the end.

I brute-forced every MC key and every short answer (`itertools` enumeration, `math.comb`). All agree with the school's printed answer except James Ruse 2020 Q14(c) (row F4), which is flagged.

Tags follow the spec topic map: 14A factorial algebra; 14B arrangements, nPr, digits and numbers, with restrictions; 14C grouping (together/apart, blocks, alternating); 14D identical elements (letters with repeats, grid paths); 14E combinations, nCr, committees, selections with restrictions, nCr identities from counting; 14F probability via counting. A row tagged "14C/14D" fits either lesson.
