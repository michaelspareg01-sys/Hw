## Past-paper catalogue: Year 12 Ext 1 Integration (Parts 1–3)

All paths are relative to `workspace/pp/Ext 1/`. "p" is the PDF page number, not the printed page. I read every question page of every school paper below as a rendered image (the text layers are often garbled or missing), so the question numbers, marks and wording come from the paper itself. Solution pages were found by eye; where a file has no solutions the entry says "none". For many handwritten sets the solution page is the page where that question's solution starts.

**Papers swept (2020–2024, school trials only):** 2020 Abbotsleigh, Ascham, Baulkham Hills, Blacktown Boys, Caringbah, Cherrybrook, Fort Street, Girraween, Hornsby Girls, Killara, Manly, NSB, NSG, Normanhurst Boys, SBHS (trial and the separate sample paper), SGGHS, SGHS, Sydney Tech Samples 1–3, James Ruse, Sydney Grammar; 2021 Caringbah, Girraween, Hurlstone, Killara, King's, Normanhurst Boys, NSB, Sydney Grammar, Sydney Tech, Abbotsleigh; 2022 Blacktown Boys, Gosford, Hornsby Girls, Knox, Normanhurst Boys, NSB, Sydney Grammar; 2023 Kinross Wolaroi, Killara, Abbotsleigh, Ascham, Baulkham Hills, Blacktown Boys, Girraween, Gosford, Hornsby Girls, Hurlstone, Knox, Moriah, NSB, Normanhurst Boys, NSG, PLC, James Ruse, SGHS, Sydney Grammar, Sydney Tech; 2024 Abbotsleigh, Ascham, Blacktown Boys, Killara, PLC, Saint Ignatius, St Aloysius, Epping Boys.

**Not swept (as the brief says):** NEAP, PEM, TEC, CSSA, Independent and the BoS zip; `2021/3U JRAHS.pdf` (2021 mock), `2020/2020 Western.pdf`, `2023/Fort_Street_2023_Extension_1__Solutions.pdf` and `2024/NSB 3U.pdf`. `2020/2020 Ruse.pdf` is a marked student copy of James Ruse 2020 and `2024/Killara 3U.pdf` is a garbled scan of Killara 2024, so I used the clean files instead.

**Scope.** Primitives, definite and indefinite integrals, areas, the trapezoidal rule, the reverse chain rule, exponential, logarithmic and trigonometric integrals, derivatives and integrals of the inverse trigonometric functions, sin² and cos² and products-to-sums integrals, substitution, and volumes of rotation. I left out differential equations (Ch 13), probability density functions, motion (velocity–time graphs), related rates, and inverse-trig items that only ask for a domain, range, sketch or exact value (Y11). Where a question mixes these, only the integration parts are listed.

**Tags.** Each row carries the EARLIEST lesson (spec lesson table: int1-L1 … int1-L10, int2-L1 … int2-L7, int3-L1 … int3-L6) whose methods are enough for the whole row. Rows are grouped (i)(ii) only when the later part uses the earlier one; otherwise parts with different tags have separate rows. Where a question prescribes a method ("using the substitution …") the tag is the lesson that teaches that method, even if an earlier method would also work. Linear substitutions (u = 1 + x, u = 2x + 1) and u = √(…) substitutions are tagged int3-L4 (12D); x = (function of u) substitutions, including x = sin θ, x = tan θ and x = (u − 4)² + 1, are tagged int3-L5 (12E). Inverse-trig derivative items go to int3-L1 even when they are "show that" or tangent/normal items. A volume that also needs a later integration skill (sin², e^x, 1/x, tan²) is still tagged int3-L6, the last lesson, with the skill in the tag note.

**ALREADY USED** was checked by script and by hand against `docs/PAST_PAPERS_USED.md` (including its abbreviations such as "Abb20 Q2/Q11c", "BBHS22 Q11a/d/e", "SydTech20 sample1 Q11c"), every `docs/pp_selected_*.md`, and the source labels printed in every built pack (`workspace/build/*/`, which give the exact part, e.g. "BBHS 2022 Q11(a)(ii)"). The used label "SBHS20" refers to the real trial (`2020 SBHS 2.pdf`), not the sample paper. A row counts as used if any used label overlaps it (same question, and the same part or a part of it).

**Flags.** "Doubtful" = inconsistent wording or data, or a wrong school solution; the reason is given, and a summary list is at the end. "Needs …" or "uses (i)" = the row depends on another part, whose result can be supplied in the stem (house rule 2). Other flags record missing solutions, revision sample papers, duplicated questions, and solution files that do not match.

**What the counts mean for the three packs**

- **Part 1 is very thin (12 items; 11 clean items worth 12 marks, almost all 1-mark MC).** Ext 1 trials do not test Advanced integration for its own sake. 4J, 5D, 5E and 5G have no item at all; 5A, 5B, 5H have one MC each. Most Part 1 lessons will need house rule 8 (use what exists, at least one question, plus a timed-practice note pointing to textbook questions).
- **Part 2 is thin too (27 items, 70 clean marks).** 6J and 6K have no item whose earliest lesson they are. 7D (int2-L6) is the richest Part 2 lesson (reverse chain with trig, tan x, cot x, sec⁴ x). Many Part 3 volume items need 6D/7D skills, but their earliest lesson is still int3-L6.
- **Part 3 is rich (331 items, 665 clean marks).** Every Part 3 lesson can reach 15–22 marks of clean items several times over, so Part 3 can afford to avoid all doubtful items and all no-solution papers if it wants to.
- 48 rows are already used (47 of them in Part 3, nearly all by the Ch 12 pack). 10 rows are doubtful.
