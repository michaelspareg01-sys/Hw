## Past-paper candidates: Year 11 Ext 1 Chapter 6 Trigonometry, sections 6G to 6K

Pool: the school trial papers in `workspace/pp/Ext 1/2020` to `2024`. All paths below are relative to `pp/Ext 1/`. "p" is the PDF page number. Left out: every commercial paper (CSSA, NEAP, PEM, TEC, Independent, the BoS zip), `2020/2020 Western.pdf`, `2021/3U JRAHS.pdf` (2021 mock), `2023/Fort_Street_2023...`, `2024/NSB 3U.pdf`, and the doubtful items listed in the y11ch13 spec.

### How the pool was searched
1. Text layer: every page of every school paper was searched for sine rule, cosine rule, bearing, elevation, depression, tower, triangle, cosec, sec, cot, identity, degrees and the degree sign. Each hit was read in context.
2. Scans: the 534 pages with no text layer (the whole of Baulkham Hills 2020, Manly 2020, NSB 2020, Ruse 2020, Girraween 2021, NSB 2021, Knox 2022, Moriah 2023 and PLC 2024, plus the scanned pages of the other papers) were run through OCR (tesseract) and searched for the same words. Every hit was read.
3. Each possible item was then checked against the scope of 6G to 6K and the methods taught up to that section (Year 11 book Chapters 1 to 6; radians come in 11G, compound angles, double angles and the t-formulae in 17D to 17F, vectors and forces in Year 12).

### Result
Year 12 trial papers test the Year 11 Chapter 6 skills almost only inside Year 12 topics (vectors, forces, projectiles, t-formulae, auxiliary angles). Two clean items fit 6G to 6K, both in 6G. House rule 8 applies to 6H, 6I, 6J and 6K: no clean school item exists for them.
