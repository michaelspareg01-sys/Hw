# Year 8 Congruence: past-paper candidates (Y9 and Y10 yearly exams)

Source folders: `workspace/pp/Y9/` (21 papers) and `workspace/pp/Y10/` (40 papers). Every page of all 61 files was rendered with PyMuPDF and looked at in contact sheets (8 pages per sheet). Each candidate page was then re-rendered at reading size. As a second net, the text layer of every page was searched, and so was a tesseract OCR of every page without a text layer (780 pages). The search terms were congru, ≡, SSS, SAS, AAS, ASA, RHS, isosceles, parallelogram, rhombus, kite, bisect, prove and corresponding. Page numbers are 1-based PDF page indices, not printed page numbers.

**Lesson tags.** L1 = congruent figures, corresponding parts and notation (8D). L2 = the tests SSS, SAS, AAS and RHS, naming a test and writing a statement (8E). L3 = written congruence proofs, "hence" parts that use a proved congruence, and proofs of isosceles or quadrilateral properties (8H). Short proofs (2–3 marks, no "hence" part) are tagged L3 but could also serve as hard L2 items. The Notes column says where that applies.

**Flags.**
- EXCLUDED: publisher recreation (Sydney Boys 2025).
- ALREADY USED: the item is in a `docs/pp_selected_*.md` file.
- WRONG SOLUTION: the printed solution is wrong.
- SOLUTION SLIP: the printed solution has a typo or a mislabelled reason, but its answer is right.
- NO SOLUTIONS: the file has no solutions.
- NEEDS TRIG / NEEDS CIRCLE: the item also uses a method outside the Y8 course.
- MARKS NOT PRINTED: the paper does not print per-part marks.

No item is DOUBTFUL.
